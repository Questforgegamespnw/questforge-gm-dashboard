# Lantern House Secret

The old beacon was changed by the missing lamplighter. He tried to build a light that could find sailors lost in supernatural fog.

It worked.

Then the light learned that names were coordinates.

The lamp can now locate people through names, lies, blood, and memory. It is not purely malicious. It is a wounded tool with a fragment of the missing lamplighter bound inside it, and something from the fog pushing through the same opening.

The party's final choice should not be a simple correct answer. Each path saves something and risks something.

- Dim the lamp: safer now, but the missing may fade.
- Feed the lamp: more truth, more danger.
- Redirect the beam: reveals the fog, draws attention.
- Break the lens: ends the lure, but strands the unresolved dead/lost.
