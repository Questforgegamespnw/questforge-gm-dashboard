# Act 1 Perception vs Truth Chart

Use this when improvising. If unsure what to say, speak from Player Perception or Observed Weirdness. Never speak directly from DM Truth.

## Arrival — Skjoldyr's Hollow

Player Perception: a normal settlement under threat.

Observed Weirdness: NPCs hesitate, things feel slightly too controlled, small environmental inconsistencies.

DM Truth: the area is under system strain; the Root is stabilizing reality beneath them.

Goal: something feels off, but nothing is clearly wrong yet.

## First Disturbance

Player Perception: something attacked the village.

Observed Weirdness: creatures behave inconsistently, repeat motions, and fail to react normally to pain.

DM Truth: these are unresolved dead pulled by system imbalance.

Goal: normal combat becomes strange combat.

## Root Discovery

Player Perception: the root is under attack or the source of the problem.

Observed Weirdness: the root feels active, not damaged. Reality pauses or behaves incorrectly.

DM Truth: the root is stabilizing and compensating.

Goal: introduce doubt.

## Siege / Rift Event

Player Perception: this is the main event.

Observed Weirdness: the fight feels too structured, waves feel timed, enemies ignore optimal tactics, pressure escalates regardless of success.

DM Truth: the siege is a distraction or containment mechanism while a process at the Root has already occurred or is completing.

Goal: this fight matters, but something else is going on.

## Post-Siege

Player Perception: the town is saved.

Observed Weirdness: bodies do not resolve correctly, frost persists, environment remains wrong.

DM Truth: death is not resolving correctly and the Root is still compensating.

Goal: victory without closure.

## Root Shrine

Player Perception: we arrived just in time or stopped something.

Observed Weirdness: shrine is untouched, runes are complete, effects feel residual and already settled.

DM Truth: the process completed, partially completed, or was altered by player action. They did not fully stop it.

Goal: “Wait… did we stop anything?”

## Execution Rule

Never say “ritual completed” too cleanly unless the players earn the inference. Use timing mismatch, persistent symptoms, NPC uncertainty, and consequences.
