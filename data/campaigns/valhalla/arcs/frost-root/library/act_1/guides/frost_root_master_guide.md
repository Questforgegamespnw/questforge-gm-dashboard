# DM Master Guide — Act 1: The Frost Root

> Run the lie cleanly so the truth lands later.

## What Act 1 Actually Is

Act 1 is a controlled success story. Players should feel powerful, effective, and needed.

Underneath that, the system is already breaking.

You are not running a survival arc or a mystery arc. You are running a stability illusion.

## Three Layers of Reality

Every scene has three layers:

1. Player Reality: “We are winning. We are stopping threats. We are heroes.”
2. Observed Weirdness: patterns do not match expectations; NPCs hesitate; systems behave slightly wrong.
3. DM Truth: the system is overcorrecting, the Root is reacting, and death is being interfered with.

Never expose Layer 3 in Act 1.

## Critical Beats

- Ridge Reveal: reframes the siege as intentional, not random.
- Runecaller Entrance: introduces awareness, control, inevitability.
- Rift Moment: introduces cosmic scale.
- Heroic Recovery: players believe they fixed the immediate danger.
- Quiet / aftermath / Root Shrine: introduces doubt.

## Core Systems to Run Cleanly

Yggdrasilmaed enables hard encounters and risk/reward pressure. Encourage use, but never make it feel free.

Fear should influence movement, not lock players out.

Nodes split focus and add objectives. They are pressure tools, not primary threats.

Resonance Clash is a dramatic Bard-vs-Skald moment, not a heavy mechanical shutdown.

Ragnarok Meter starts at 2. Full success keeps it at 2; partial success moves to 3; failure moves to 4.

Items scale with Ragnarok tier, not level.

## Encounter Design Rules

Pressure, not punishment. Enemies constrain, reposition, and escalate. They do not hard-counter builds or instantly delete players.

Every role matters:

- Bard: control / clash / Rift song
- Rogue: objectives / discovery / node dive
- Paladin: anchor / defense / fear lockdown
- Corewright: system interaction / golem anchor

Escalation should layer: control → pressure → collapse.

## NPC Usage Rules

NPCs hold lines, react, and reinforce tone. They do not win fights.

Valkyries are unified and confident in Act 1. Only Kara-type cracks should show later if needed.

Heimdall observes, confirms, and warns. He never explains.

## What Not To Do

Do not reveal the truth early. Do not explain Root mechanics. Do not make Valhalla unsafe yet. Do not remove player agency.

## End State

At the end of Act 1, players should feel:

- We saved the town.
- That fight was intense.
- We are part of something bigger.

But also:

- Something felt off.
- That did not fully resolve.
- We may not understand what happened.

End Act 1 like a pause, not an ending.
