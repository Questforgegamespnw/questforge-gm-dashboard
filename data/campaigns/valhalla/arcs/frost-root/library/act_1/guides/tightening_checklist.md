# Final Act 1 Tightening Checklist

Run this before moving into Act 2.

## 1. Frost Root Language

No NPC clearly calls it corrupted as truth. No text confirms it is failing. Use uncertain, observational, interpretive language.

Good: “Something is happening to it.” “It’s reacting.” “It’s not behaving normally.”

Avoid: “The root is dying.” “The root is corrupted.”

## 2. Siege Is Not Full Story

The fight is important and intense, but too structured. Wave timing feels intentional. Enemies sometimes ignore optimal tactics. Pressure continues even when players succeed.

## 3. Process Already Happened

At the Root Shrine, signs are already complete or residual. Energy feels settled, cycling, or looping, not building.

Use line:

> This… this wasn’t just starting.

## 4. Draugr Consistency

They are not angry, tactical, or reactive. They are purposeful, incomplete, and disconnected.

If players ask why they attack:

> They aren’t attacking you. They’re doing something you’re in the way of.

## 5. Post-Siege Does Not Fully Resolve

Bodies remain or behave incorrectly. Environment still feels wrong. NPCs are relieved but uneasy.

## 6. NPC Mystery Discipline

Jarl is practical. Torvald notices something grounded. Yrsa is closest to truth but incomplete. Valkyries stay confident and unified.

No one says “this is a system issue” or “this is stabilizing reality.”

## 7. Heimdall Consistency

Short statements only. Calm, absolute, ominous. Silence is allowed.

## 8. Three-Layer System

Every scene needs player clarity, weirdness, and implied hidden truth.

If weirdness is missing, add a timing issue, behavior mismatch, or environmental oddity.

## 9. Discovery Shift

By the end, players should believe they saved the town, feel something was off, and begin to suspect they do not understand what happened.

If they feel they solved the problem cleanly, add one unresolved element.

## 10. Final Line Check

Act 1 should feel like a pause, not an ending.
