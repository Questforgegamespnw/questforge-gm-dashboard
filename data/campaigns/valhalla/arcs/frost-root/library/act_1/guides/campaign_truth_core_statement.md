# Campaign Truth — Core Statement

> This world is not breaking. It is being forced to correct something that should not exist.

This is a DM-only truth document. Do not read it during session. Use it to stay consistent while improvising.

## Primary Conflict

A system designed to manage death, continuity, and reality is encountering something outside its design.

## Yggdrasil — Reality Engine

Yggdrasil is not just a tree. It is:

- a reality-regulation system
- a continuity stabilizer
- a multi-layer existence framework

It manages life/death flow, soul routing, timeline consistency, and realm separation.

Current state: under strain, overcorrecting, and compensating for an unknown anomaly.

## The Frost Root

The Frost Root is a localized stabilization node, pressure regulator, and failsafe.

It is not corrupted, failing, or hostile.

It is preventing collapse, slowing imbalance, and holding back something larger.

Side effects:

- death does not resolve cleanly
- bodies persist
- frost lingers
- reality behaves inconsistently

## Valhalla — War Engine Disguised as Afterlife

Valhalla is not merely a reward. It is a recruitment and recycling system, combat-readiness engine, and controlled loop of death and return.

The hidden problem: Valhalla assumes the system it supports is stable. It is not.

## The Anomaly

Something exists that should not be in the system, cannot be categorized, and cannot be processed.

Known effects:

- timeline inconsistencies
- repetition / “again” moments
- detection failures
- partial returns

## The Players

The players are not fully native to the system, not behaving like normal souls, and not fully accounted for.

They may be outside the cycle, from a different iteration, or part of the instability itself. Do not reveal early.

## Draugr

Draugr are unresolved dead: souls that failed to process correctly, system overflow, unfinished instructions.

They attack because they are drawn to stabilization points and pulled by system pressure, not because they are malicious.

## The Rift

The Rift is a pressure release point and symptom, not the cause.

Closing it solves immediate danger but not the underlying issue.

## The Maker

The Maker is not a god, not part of the system, and not bound by it. It observes, adjusts, and rewrites.

The Maker is aware of the players in a way the system is not.

## Loki / Balder Thread

Balder may not be what he appears. Loki may be embedded, interfering, or exploiting instability.

Do not confirm early. Let suspicion build.

## Core Illusion

The campaign presents a heroic defense against chaos.

The truth: the players are intervening in a system attempting to stabilize itself.

## Running Rules

Never explain the system fully, confirm the Root’s true role in Act 1, reveal the anomaly directly, or let NPCs solve the mystery.

Always reinforce success, add subtle inconsistencies, let outcomes feel slightly unresolved, and maintain player agency.

## Long-Term Payoff

At a future reveal, players should realize:

> We didn’t just fight the problem. We may have been part of it.
