# Yrsa Root-Seer — She Who Listens to the Sap

> You are not meant to stay.

## Core Identity

Mortal conduit to the World Tree root. Not a traditional druid; more like a listener who has started hearing something new.

## Appearance

Pale, gaunt, layered furs and bark-thread cloth. Eyes covered with a sap-stained amber veil. Fingers permanently stained gold with hardened resin. Root and twig fragments woven into clothing.

## Presence

The air near her feels quieter. Not safe—listened to.

## Personality

Calm, never hurried, cryptic, and always slightly ahead. She speaks as if answering something else first. Her metaphors are roots, sap, growth, and rot.

## Role

- deliver truth sideways
- foreshadow Root mechanics
- confirm stakes without explaining everything
- sense nodes, Rift, Runecaller, and unnatural dead

## Dialogue Bank

- “You came down wrong… not like the others.”
- “You still carry the sky with you.”
- “It remembers everything that has ever touched it… and now something new has.”
- “These are not the dead. These are… instructions.”
- “The root is not sick… it is being taught to rot.”
- “Something has learned how to wear death.”
- “It does not speak to the root. It writes into it.”
- “...it has found a way to speak louder.”

## Interaction Notes

Yrsa may react before players notice something:

> It has begun again...

She can sense the party is not mortal-bound and that they are loaned / system-strange souls, but she will not explain cleanly.

If pressed, Insight DC ~14 reveals she is afraid, but not for herself.

## Optional Support

Sap Ward: once, bless a 10-foot area. Allies gain advantage on next save vs fear.

Root Guidance: grants advantage on Arcana, Nature, or Religion related to Root phenomena.

She helps others act. She does not fix problems herself.
