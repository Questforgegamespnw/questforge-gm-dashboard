# Torvald Brewmaster — Keeper of the Sap

> If it’s weak, we fix it.

## Core Identity

Master brewer of Yggdrasil sap-mead and keeper of the living root tap beneath the Sap-Hearth. Not a soldier, but refuses to abandon the town.

## Role

- emotional anchor
- stakes reinforcement
- practical problem-solver
- early visible indicator that sap flow is weakening
- anchor point for the Sap-Hearth

## Appearance

Massive, broad-shouldered, built like a siege engine. Soot-covered face. Braided beard with frost and ash. Leather apron over dented armor. Heavy forge hammer and half-full tankard.

Smells of smoke, sap, and strong ale.

## Presence

Heat hits you before the man does. The mead hall still burns warm against the cold, and at its center stands someone who refuses to let it die.

## Personality

Loud, blunt, stubborn, zero patience for nonsense. Deeply loyal to town, root, and craft.

Core belief:

> If the root dies, we all do.

## Dialogue Bank

- “If the root dies, we all do—so get moving!”
- “You look like you can fight. Good. You’re needed.”
- “You—machine-maker—tell me that thing can hold a line!”
- “This isn’t drink—this is lifeblood. You waste it, you answer to me.”
- “You don’t take from the root. You ask—and you take only what it gives.”
- “This isn’t a raid. This is someone trying to take the whole damn world with it.”
- “She hears things I don’t want to believe. So I keep brewing—and hope she’s wrong.”
- “You want me to run? Then you better be ready to carry the root with you.”

## Mead Hall / Sap-Hearth

The Sap-Hearth is central to the town, built over a root tap chamber. It houses sap collection, fermentation vats, and sacred brewing process.

If destroyed, morale collapses immediately.

## Combat Use

Torvald may hold a choke point or protect others, but he should not chase enemies or solve the encounter.

## Clue Use

Torvald may call the disturbance poisoning because he is grounded and practical. Hidden truth remains more complex: redirection, strain, teaching, and interference.
