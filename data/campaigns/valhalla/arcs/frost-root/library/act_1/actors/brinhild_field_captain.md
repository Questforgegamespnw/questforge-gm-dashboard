# Brinhild — Field Captain

> Hold the line or die tired.

## Overview

Battlefield Valkyrie present during the siege of Skjoldyr's Hollow.

She is a set-piece ally, not a DMPC.

## Presence

- frost-covered armor
- shield-first fighter
- always mid-motion
- aggressive, tactical, decisive

She treats players like soldiers, not heroes.

## Role

- hold a critical lane
- buy time
- demonstrate elite combat
- call tactical lines

She supports. She does not solve the fight.

## Barks

- “Hold the line or die tired.”
- “You’re late. Good. That means you’re needed.”
- “Then we kill them inside the walls instead!”
- “Push them back!”
- “You hold HERE!”
- “...so. You finally show yourself.”

## Best Moment

Let her take a massive hit and hold anyway:

> She plants her shield. The impact echoes. But she does not move.

## Do / Don't

Do: knock enemies back, protect weaker NPCs, call tactical lines.

Do not: kill bosses, solve objectives, outshine player turns.

## Cleanup Note

Older draft stat blocks used Svinhild's name in some feature text. Final use here should read Brinhild. Svinhild remains the Valhalla commander; Brinhild is the Act 1 field captain.
