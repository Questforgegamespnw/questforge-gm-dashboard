# Jarl Eirik Skjoldrson — The Wall That Walks

> We don’t win this—we survive it.

## Core Identity

Veteran war leader of Skjoldyr's Hollow. He has survived battles that should have killed him and does not believe in prophecy, destiny, or divine rescue.

Core belief:

> You don’t win wars. You outlast them.

## Appearance

Broad, scarred, late fifties, still imposing. Thick gray beard with worn iron rings. One eye clouded and useless. Mismatched repaired plates, heavy gouged shield, battle-worn axe.

## Presence

He does not move like a hero. He moves like something that has refused to die for too long to remember how.

## Personality

Blunt. No wasted words. No patience for hesitation. Not cruel, not gentle. Respects action over intent.

## Role

- battlefield anchor
- mortal contrast to Valkyries
- “hold the line” embodiment
- practical post-siege survival pressure

## Dialogue Bank

- “If you’re here to help, then stop watching and start killing!”
- “Hold your ground! They break before we do!”
- “We don’t win this—we survive it!”
- “You fight like something that’s already died once.”
- “Fear’s fine. Just don’t let it choose where you stand.”
- “These things don’t tire. Good thing we don’t need to last forever—just longer than them.”
- “...there it is. The reason this isn’t over yet.”

## Battlefield Use

If gate intact, he holds the choke point.

If gate breached, he falls back to the inner line or crossroads and becomes the last line of resistance.

## Design Purpose

Jarl is the line anchor. The Gate Captain is just one of the soldiers on the wall.

He is not flashy, magical, or clever. He is immovable.

Best moment:

> He plants his shield. The ground cracks beneath it. He does not move.
