# Frostwake / Hollow Defenders

## How to Use Defenders in Combat

Do:

- have them engaged already
- let players save them occasionally
- use them to anchor positions

Do not:

- track them like PCs
- let them overshadow players
- keep them alive too long when tension needs escalation

Best use case:

> Defenders are environmental storytelling with hit points.

Arrival moment:

> The defenders are already losing. Not panicking. Not routing. Just... losing.

## Unit Roles

### Hollow Guard / Frostwake Defender

Purpose: hold lines briefly, die fast against elites.

Use shield wall behavior and show competence without solving the scene.

### Hollow Archer / Frostwake Archer

Purpose: wall pressure, harass enemies, then get overwhelmed if the wall is breached.

### Frostwake Gate Captain

Purpose: local wall command voice and saveable competent soldier. Not the line anchor. Jarl Eirik is the line anchor.

Use barks:

- “They’re breaking through!”
- “We can’t hold this!”
- “Archers—left wall!”
