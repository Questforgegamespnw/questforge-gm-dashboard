# Rift Core — The Wound

> It is not a doorway. It is a mistake.

## Role

Escalation engine and chaos source.

## First Sight

The air tears. Not with sound, but with absence. Light bends toward it, snow lifts from the ground, and the world around it seems to forget how to stay still.

## Active State

Things step out of it that do not arrive; they simply become present. Distance stops meaning anything near it. Sound warps. Motion stutters.

It is not a doorway. It is a mistake that has not been corrected.

## Collapse

It does not close. It folds, like a wound remembering it was never meant to be open.

## Quick Rules

- AC 14
- HP 60 baseline
- 15-foot Reality Tear aura
- initiative 20 spawns Draugr
- optional first two rounds spawn two if needed
- Close the Wound: three DC 15 Arcana, Religion, or Performance successes adjacent to Rift

## Spotlight

Rogue dives into the Rift pressure.

Paladin holds the line.

Corewright golem anchors the tear.

Bard can perform the Rift Song.

## Final Rule

The Rift is not trying to kill them. It is trying to stay open.
