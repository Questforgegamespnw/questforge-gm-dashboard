# Corruption Nodes / Local Wrongness Nodes

> The world isn’t breaking. It’s being rewritten... locally.

“Corruption Node” is acceptable tactical shorthand, but lore-facing truth is more specific: these are localized fractures of Root stability, points where reality is being incorrectly maintained.

## Role

- battlefield control zones
- enemy buff sources
- player objective targets

## Quick Stats

- AC 13
- HP 25
- medium object
- immune to poison and psychic
- standard object condition immunities

## Aura

10-foot radius:

- difficult terrain for players / enemies of creator
- allied undead gain +1d4 cold damage on first hit each turn

## Pulse of Ruin

Triggered by Runecaller activation or when reduced below half HP.

Effect: undead within range reroll active fear saves with advantage.

## Player Interactions

Destroy: attacks remove node permanently.

Suppress: action, DC 15 Arcana or Religion, disables aura for one round.

Rogue play: dash, disable, reposition.

Corewright play: golem anchors node or interacts under pressure.

Paladin play: hold center while others solve.

## Description Lines

- “The snow gathers... but not randomly.”
- “Something beneath the ground is breathing.”
- “The air tightens when you step closer.”

## Pulse Description

The air tightens. For a heartbeat, everything feels closer, heavier—the whipping snow turning into jagged ice and falling hard. The dead around you seem to remember something they should not.

## Destruction

It does not explode. It unravels, the light collapsing inward, leaving behind snow that finally falls the way it should.

## DM Rules

Use nodes to split the party, force movement, and create priority tension. Do not stack too tightly or hide them from players.
