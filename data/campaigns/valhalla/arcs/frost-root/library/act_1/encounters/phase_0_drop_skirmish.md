# Encounter Dossier — Phase 0: The Drop Skirmish

> The ground was already wrong before you arrived.

## Purpose

This encounter is not about danger. It is about tone, mystery, player positioning, and foreshadowing the process. The real fight has not started yet.

## Opening Description

The sky splits in radiant color as you fall—  
Light becomes heat, heat becomes frost—  
You strike the earth, snow erupting outward.  
Silence follows.  
Then—  
The snow here isn’t untouched.  
Something has passed through—  
dragging, shifting, breaking the surface in long, uneven lines.  
Crates lie half-buried, abandoned or dropped in haste.  
And the wind…  
doesn’t quite cover the tracks.

## Enemy Staging

Do not place everything at once.

Draugr:

- two emerge from snow drifts
- one rises near the marking site
- one drags a corpse

Frost Stalker, if used, circles wide or stays behind outcrop as sound before sight.

Skald is not used yet.

## Behavior

For at least one round, draugr ignore players and continue the task: dragging bodies, carving runes, placing corpses deliberately.

Realization line:

> They are not attacking. They are building something.

They attack when interfered with or after one full round.

## Perception Rewards

Passive 10–14: tracks, drag marks, uneven ground.

Passive 15+: organized movement, bodies placed intentionally, blue glow beneath snow.

Passive 20+ or active investigation:

> The pattern reveals itself to you. The draugr are not attacking at random. They are building something. The markings in the snow form a rough triangle. Each point glows faintly beneath the surface. The bodies they drag are being placed precisely along those lines.

Optional whisper:

> As you focus, something in the pattern focuses back. Not eyes. Not awareness. Just the sense that you’ve been noticed.

## End Condition

When enemies fall or retreat, wind picks up and silence returns briefly.

Bridge:

> Beyond the ridge—something larger is happening.

Then run the Ridge Reveal.
