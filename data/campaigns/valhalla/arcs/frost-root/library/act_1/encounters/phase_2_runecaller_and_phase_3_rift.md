# Encounter Dossier — Phase 2 Runecaller + Phase 3 Rift

## Phase 2 — The Runecaller Arrives

> The battle doesn’t escalate. It gets directed.

### Purpose

Transform chaotic survival into controlled pressure and boss presence. The players realize the battle is orchestrated.

### Entry

The wind dies.  
Not fades—dies.  
For a single breath, the battlefield stops resisting itself.  
Then—something steps through the storm.  
Not rushing.  
Not searching.  
Knowing.  
Runes burn beneath its feet—and the dead move differently.  
And then—it looks at you.

Pause. Place miniature/token.

### What Changed

Before: enemies were behavior-driven and chaos was environmental.

Now: enemies are directed, battlefield is controlled, players are being responded to.

### Runecaller Role

Not a brute. Not a caster. A battlefield controller.

It activates nodes, repositions, punishes clustering, enables undead, and protects the process.

### Mechanics

On entry, trigger an immediate pulse: undead reroll fear saves with advantage, suppressed nodes may reactivate, and pressure spikes.

Legendary-lite at end of player turns, 2 per round total:

- Ice Lash: ranged chip damage, backline/low HP pressure
- Rune Shift: teleport/reposition 15 ft
- Pulse Corruption: costs 2, activates node or battlefield shift

Command the Dead creates chain movement or attacks.

### Skald + Runecaller

Bard spellcasting can trigger Resonance Clash. Make it dramatic, not punitive.

## Phase 3 — The Rift Opens

> This is not a doorway. It is a mistake.

### Trigger

Runecaller at roughly 50–60% HP, or after sustained battlefield control.

### Opening

The ground fractures—  
not with force…  
but with intent.  
Light leaks upward—  
bending as it escapes—  
snow lifting, not falling.  
Sound stretches.  
Movement stutters.  
And something—  
on the other side—  
notices you.

### Rift Identity

A wound in reality. Not object, not creature, not doorway.

### Live Mechanics

- Spawn engine at initiative 20.
- Reality Tear aura around Rift.
- Void Flare periodic/triggered.
- Close the Wound: adjacent action, DC 15 Arcana, Religion, or Performance. Three successes collapse/fold the Rift.

### Spotlight Windows

Bard: the Rift Song, performance/persuasion closure.

Rogue: the dive through terrain and node chaos.

Paladin: the line, fear lock, civilian protection.

Corewright: golem anchors the tear physically.

### Transition to Recovery

When Rift folds or Runecaller dies/disconnects:

> The tear doesn’t close—it folds. Like something remembering it was never meant to be open.
