# At-Table Run Sheet — Frost Root Drop + Siege

## Objective

Hold the line, stabilize the gate, manage battlefield pressure.

## Fail State

The Root process completes without interference, the Rift overwhelms, or the gate collapses with heavy losses.

## Hidden Truth

The battle is a distraction / containment event around a completed or redirected Root process.

## Tone Arc

Chaos → Control → Overwhelm → Uneasy Victory

## DM Pressure Rule

Players are Level 10 and have Yggdrasilmaed. Push difficulty aggressively. Let them feel powerful before escalation.

## Phase 0 — Drop

One round of perception before full engagement.

Draugr ignore players while carving, dragging, and placing bodies. They engage only if interrupted or after one full round.

Mandatory transition:

> The ridge breaks—and below you—war.

Set Gate State after Phase 0: intact, cracking, or breached.

## Live Trackers

- Gate: intact / cracking / breached
- Nodes: 3 / 2 / 1 / destroyed
- Rift: not open / open / folded
- Civilians: stable / threatened / heavy losses
- Ragnarok: 2 / 3 / 4

## Phase 1 — Siege

The defenders are already losing, not panicking or routing. Players must choose between gate, civilians, nodes, Skald, and brutes.

Use Brinhild, Jarl, Gate Captain, guards, and archers as pressure anchors.

## Phase 2 — Runecaller

Mandatory entry:

> The wind dies. Not fades—dies. Then something walks through the storm. And it looks at you.

On entry: nodes flare, undead move corrected, battlefield belongs to something else.

## Phase 3 — Rift

Mandatory opening:

> The ground fractures—not with force, but with intent.

Spawn pressure begins. Close the Wound becomes available.

## Phase 4 — Recovery

Mandatory resolution:

> The tear doesn’t close—it folds.

Pause 5–10 seconds. No music. No cheers. Just breathing, wind, and distant fire.

NPC reactions:

- Brinhild: “...That should not have been here.”
- Villagers: “What... was that?”
- Yrsa: “This is not death as it should be.”

## Player Spotlight Reminders

- Paladin: hold line / anchor / fear lockdown
- Rogue: nodes / movement / disruption
- Bard: Skald duel / control / Rift song
- Corewright: physical control / anchor / system interaction
