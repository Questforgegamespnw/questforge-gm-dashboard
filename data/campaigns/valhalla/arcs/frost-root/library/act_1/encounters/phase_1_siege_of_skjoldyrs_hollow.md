# Encounter Dossier — Phase 1: Siege of Skjoldyr's Hollow

> This is not a fight. This is a line breaking.

## Purpose

Shift from “we landed” to “we are in a war.” Establish battlefield scale, split decisions, nodes as objectives, and NPC pressure.

## Ridge Reveal

> The ridge breaks—and below you—war.

## Gate State

Set after Phase 0.

Intact: defenders organized, fewer enemies inside town, Brinhild strong.

Cracking: one breach lane, civilians in danger, brute pressure increases.

Breached: enemies inside, civilians high risk, two-front fight.

## Battlefield Jobs

Players discover, rather than being told:

- hold the gate
- protect civilians
- disable nodes
- shut down Skald

They cannot do all of these at once.

## Forces

Draugr are already engaged with defenders, pushing gate, or guarding nodes.

Frostbound Brutes target structures and positions.

Skald is heard before seen and makes the battlefield move in time with its chant.

Nodes are live objectives.

## Phase Flow

### Phase 1A — Controlled Chaos

Players arrive mid-fight. Brinhild and Jarl hold pressure lines. Let players choose direction.

### Phase 1B — Skirmish Shift

Skald becomes active. Nodes become obvious.

Line:

> The chant rises—and suddenly everything feels harder to hold together.

### Phase 1C — War State

Node pulse triggers, brutes push harder, civilians become immediate pressure.

Line:

> For a moment, the battlefield doesn’t feel like yours anymore.

## Transition to Phase 2

Trigger after three rounds, player stabilization, node damage/destruction, or gate breaking point.

Entry line:

> The wind dies. Something walks through the storm.

## DM Gold

This phase should feel like players arrived too late, but not too late. They are stabilizing, not initiating.
