# Encounter Dossier — Phase 4: Heroic Recovery

> You held the line… but something got through.

This phase is not cleanup. It is release, consequence, and tone pivot.

## Purpose

Let players feel victory, decompress emotionally, reinforce cosmic unease, and transition into Act 1 RP/investigation.

Core experience: uneasy success.

## Transition

Trigger when Rift folds or Runecaller dies/disconnects.

### Boxed Text

The tear doesn’t close—  
it folds.  
Like a wound remembering  
it was never meant to be open.  
The light collapses inward.  
The air settles.  
The snow—  
finally falls  
the way it should.

Pause 5–10 seconds. No cheers. No music. Just breathing, wind, and distant fire.

## Immediate Resolution

Remaining draugr freeze, collapse, or wander briefly then fall.

Their eye-light flickers like something has decided it no longer needs them.

Nodes dim, destabilize, or go inert. One may flicker again briefly as foreshadow.

The Rift scar remains wrong: muted sound, unnatural cold, unsettled space.

## NPC Reactions

Brinhild lowers weapon slowly and says:

> ...That should not have been here.

Villagers:

> What... was that?

Yrsa:

> This is not death as it should be.

Torvald:

> ...That ain’t right.

## Outcome Tracking

Full success: gate intact/stabilized, civilians mostly safe, Rift folded. Ragnarok stays 2.

Partial success: gate damaged, some loss, Rift folded. Ragnarok moves 3.

Failure: gate breached, heavy losses, or unstable Rift aftermath. Ragnarok moves 4.

## Bridge to RP

> The battle is over. But the town is not at peace.

Options:

- follow civilians to Sap-Hearth
- inspect battlefield / nodes / Rift site
- speak with Brinhild or guards
- investigate gate damage

Use 1–2 foreshadow drops max.
