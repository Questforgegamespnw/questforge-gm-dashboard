# Post-Siege Dossier — Skjoldyr's Hollow After the Battle

> You saved them. That doesn’t mean they’re safe.

## Purpose

Decompression, investigation, social anchoring, and foreshadow delivery.

Players transition from reaction to curiosity.

## Opening

The battle is over.  
The dead no longer move.  
The fire still burns.  
And the town—  
does not celebrate.

Sensory layer:

- snow falls normally again
- smoke lingers low
- wood creaks under stress
- people speak quietly

Tone: relief without joy.

## Player Freedom

Do not guide too hard. Wherever players go becomes the main scene.

Choices:

- the Gate
- the Sap-Hearth
- Root-adjacent signs
- civilians
- battlefield / node sites

## The Gate

Visual: splintered timber, frozen blood on iron bands, bodies near breach.

Players learn the attack was sustained and intentional. Draugr targeted specific points. Brutes focused structures, not people.

Guard lines:

> We’ve fought them before… not like this.  
> They didn’t break… they pushed.

## The Sap-Hearth

Warm light against cold outside. People gather, but quietly. Sap drips slower than it should.

Torvald:

> Drink up. You’ve earned it.  
> ...Used to flow faster.

## Yrsa Root-Seer

Opening line:

> You smell like sap… and stormfire. You are not meant to stay.

She may say:

- the root is disturbed
- something is pulling wrong
- the dead are reacting to it

She does not explain Ragnarok, the Runecaller, or full system truth.

## Jarl Eirik

He provides trust earned through action, practical survival pressure, defenses, losses, and concern over another attack.

He does not care about cosmic theory. He cares about survival.

## Information Distribution

Guards: “This is different.”

Torvald: “The sap / supply / local system is strained.”

Yrsa: “Something is wrong.”

Jarl: “We need to survive.”

Players connect dots. Do not info dump.

## Hook to Root Shrine

Yrsa:

> The root is not quiet.

Jarl:

> There’s something out there. Fix it.

Torvald:

> Whatever’s wrong with the sap, it’s coming from upstream.

## Strong End Beat

The town quiets.  
The fires dim.  
The snow continues to fall.  

And somewhere—  
deep beneath the earth—  
something pulses.  

Once.  
Then again.
