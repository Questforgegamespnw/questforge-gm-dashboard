# Root Shrine — Option B: They Were Too Late

> The ritual didn’t fail. It finished.

## Core Reveal

The siege was never the true objective. It was containment, distraction, and timing.

Players should slowly understand:

- the shrine is not under attack
- the process is not ongoing
- the effect is not forming
- it is already done

## Approach — The Wrong Quiet

The path to the shrine is untouched.  
No tracks.  
No struggle.  
No sign that anything fought its way here.  
That alone should stop them.

If players call this out, reward them immediately:

> Yeah… this doesn’t match the battlefield at all.

## Shrine Entry

The shrine stands open.  
Not broken.  
Not breached.  
Allowed.  
The air is still.  
Too still.  
And the root—  
is already glowing.

## Root State

- gold light, but unstable
- old runes cracked
- new runes complete
- energy feels settled, cycling, residual

The horror is that the process succeeded and stabilized itself.

## Aftershock Instability

Every 2–3 minutes or narrative beat, choose one subtle effect:

- low rumble
- light flicker
- sound delay
- visual doubling
- player misstep / five-foot shift
- minor necrotic tick

Subtle, not combat-focused.

## Investigation Payoff

The ritual pattern suggests bodies were placed here, then removed. Meaning: they built it here and executed it remotely or completed it before the visible siege resolved.

Rune analysis:

- old runes stabilize
- new runes redirect

Not simple corruption. Reprogramming / teaching / rewriting.

Land this line only when earned:

> This wasn’t an attempt to break the root. It was an attempt—to teach it something new.

## Optional Echo Moment

If a player touches the root or stabilizes a rune, they see the battlefield, bodies, and process from above.

Like something else was watching it happen.

## Player Response Options

Cleanse: hard skill challenge, partial success at best.

Stabilize: suppresses symptoms temporarily.

Study: learn more and gain future advantage.

Reward: Root Pattern Recognition. Advantage on future root/node checks or bonus against node interactions.

## Exit Scene

As you step away, the root settles.  
The light stabilizes.  
For a moment, it looks whole.  
Then—just beneath the surface—  
you see it shift.  
Not breaking.  
Adjusting.

## Final Rule

Do not give them something to fight. Give them something to realize.
