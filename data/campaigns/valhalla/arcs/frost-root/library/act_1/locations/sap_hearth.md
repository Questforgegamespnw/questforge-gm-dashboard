# The Sap-Hearth

> The heart of the town does not beat—it seeps.

## Overview

The social and emotional core of Skjoldyr's Hollow, built around a living fragment of Yggdrasil's root.

It collects, blesses, and brews sap into sacred sustenance connected to the same divine logic as Yggdrasilmaed.

## Features

- massive central root embedded in the floor
- golden sap slowly dripping into a carved basin
- warm firelight against the harsh exterior
- roof beams twist like growing branches
- channels carved into stone guide liquid into vats

## Hidden Truth

Sap flow is weakening and changing consistency. This is the earliest grounded symptom of Root disturbance.

## Roleplay Function

- post-siege decompression
- Torvald clue delivery
- Yrsa insight delivery
- Jarl practical survival pressure if he enters
- emotional reset that still feels slightly unsettled

## Tone

Players should feel safe, warm, and slightly unsettled.

## Escalation

Tier 2: sap less potent.

Tier 3: sap discolored or inconsistent.

Tier 4: sap harmful or unusable.

## DM Notes

Avoid making it a monster room unless future prep intentionally changes course. Its main function is emotional and investigative.
