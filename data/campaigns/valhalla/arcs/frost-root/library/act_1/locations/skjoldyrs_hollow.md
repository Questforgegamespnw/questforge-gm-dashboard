# Skjoldyr's Hollow

> Where the living unknowingly guard the path between realms.

## Overview

Skjoldyr's Hollow is not merely a frontier settlement. It is a cosmic pressure valve built around a minor exposed root of Yggdrasil.

It serves three hidden purposes:

- stabilizing a Root access point
- allowing limited controlled inter-realm movement
- anchoring Valkyrie intervention zones

The people believe they guard a border. They are actually guarding a threshold they do not understand.

## Visual Identity

- built in a natural depression, “the Hollow”
- structures subtly lean inward toward the Root
- timber walls darkened with pitch and age
- snow gathers in unnatural spiral patterns
- wood grain twists into subtle spirals
- sap glows faint amber-gold
- air smells of pine, iron, and something ancient

## Thematic Note

This location should feel stable, but incorrectly so.

Nothing is chaotic yet. Everything is just slightly wrong.

## Playable Layout

Run the whole act from three core zones:

1. Gate & Palisade: defensive line, siege pressure, wall/gate decisions.
2. Sap-Hearth: social heart, Torvald, Yrsa, sap-flow clue.
3. Root Shrine: investigation/reveal anchor where they learn they were too late.

## Key Actors

Jarl Eirik Skjoldrson: practical war leader, does not believe in fate.

Yrsa Root-Seer: listens to the sap, speaks truth sideways.

Torvald Brewmaster: keeper of the sap, emotional heart and grounded clue source.

Brinhild: Valkyrie field captain holding a lane during the siege.

## Hidden Truth

The town is wrapped around something sacred it does not fully understand. If the Root threshold is redirected or forced open, Skjoldyr's Hollow becomes a breach point across realms.
