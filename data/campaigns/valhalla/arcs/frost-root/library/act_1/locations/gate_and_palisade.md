# The Gate & Palisade

> This is not a gate built to welcome—only to endure.

## Overview

The primary defensive structure and initial major combat zone of Skjoldyr's Hollow.

It is both a strategic choke point and a cosmic weak spot.

## Features

- massive timber gate reinforced with iron bands
- guard towers with horn signals
- frost clinging unnaturally to hinges
- splintered impact marks from repeated assaults
- wall walks wide enough for defenders
- low blue braziers and wind-cut snow

## Hidden Truth

The ground beneath the gate contains a root-vein. Draugr pressure is emerging from below and around the boundary, not simply beyond the wall.

## Encounter Function

Primary site for Phase 1 siege, gate state, brutes, defenders, and node introduction.

## Escalation

Tier 2: ice forms faster, ground unstable.

Tier 3: gate weakens, partial breaches.

Tier 4: gate fails entirely.

## DM Levers

Let players fortify the gate, save wall defenders, reinforce breach lanes, or turn the battle into a defense scenario.

Draugr behavior should be purposeful, not enraged.
