# Runecaller — The Director

> The battlefield belongs to me.

## Role

Tactical boss and battlefield controller.

It does not attack in anger. It directs.

## Behavior

- directs enemies
- maintains pressure
- never rushes
- never searches
- protects the process

## First Read

The battle shifts not because something stronger has arrived, but because something has decided it should. The dead find minds and frost steps a figure that does not look around because it already knows exactly where everything is.

And then it looks at you.

## Entrance Trick

Do not place the token immediately. Describe first, pause, then place.

Suggested line:

> Something steps through the tear... and suddenly the battlefield feels smaller.

## When It Pulses Nodes

The runes flare, and for a moment the battlefield feels like it belongs to something else.

## Death Beat

It does not fall. It pauses as if listening for something that no longer answers. The light in its form flickers—not fading, but disconnecting. Then it is simply gone.

## Core Rules

- Frost Rune Aura / weight aura
- Call the Frozen Dead or Command Dead
- Ice Spear / Ice Lash
- Rune Shift teleport
- Pulse Corruption / node activation
- Legendary-lite actions: 2 per round at end of player turns

## Final Rule

The Runecaller is not trying to win the fight. It is trying to complete the process.
