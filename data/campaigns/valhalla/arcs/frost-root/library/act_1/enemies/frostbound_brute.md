# Frostbound Brute — The Force

> Break it.

## Role

Structure breakers and threat anchors.

They pressure gates, walls, choke points, and player positions.

## First Sight

Something larger moves behind the line. The draugr part around it, not in fear but in recognition. It steps forward, ice cracking beneath its weight, dragging a weapon that looks less forged and more grown from winter itself.

## Combat Feel

Every swing lands like a falling tree. The ground trembles from sheer force. It does not adjust or react. It continues the motion until something breaks.

## Death Beat

It does not fall cleanly. It fractures, great cracks splitting through its body before it collapses into a heap that still looks heavy.

## Key Rules

- Siege Weight: double damage to objects and structures.
- Frozen Momentum: rewards straight-line pressure.
- Ground Smash: local knockdown and position disruption.

## Use

Use brutes to turn the gate into a clock and force Paladin/Jarl/Brinhild line-holding moments.
