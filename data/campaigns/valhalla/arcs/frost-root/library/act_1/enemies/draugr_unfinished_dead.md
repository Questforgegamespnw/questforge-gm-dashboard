# Draugr — The Unfinished Dead

> They do not stop. They do not decide. They continue.

## Role

Pressure units and objective-driven enemies.

They are unresolved dead / failed processing / system overflow, not traditional undead villains.

## Behavior

- ignore players initially
- move toward nodes, markings, bodies, or Runecaller direction
- do not panic
- do not act with malice
- do not behave like berserk undead

If frightened, they break line of sight, retreat toward node/process, or reposition. They do not flee blindly.

## First Sight

Shapes emerge through the smoke—not charging, not howling—just walking. Their armor is ancient, flesh stiff with frost, eyes burning dull patient blue.

Not rage. Purpose.

## Combat Feel

They do not flinch when struck. Blades sink in and they keep moving. One loses an arm and shifts weight as if nothing important was lost.

## Death Beat

The glow in its eyes flickers—not like a flame going out, but like something deciding it is done here.

## Table Truth

If players ask why they are attacking:

> They aren’t attacking you. They’re doing something you’re in the way of.
