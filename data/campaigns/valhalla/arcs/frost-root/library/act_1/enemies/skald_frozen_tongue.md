# Skald of the Frozen Tongue — The Counter

> Your magic is not yours alone.

## Role

Anti-caster, Bard rival, rhythm disruptor.

## First Sight

You hear it before you see it: a low chant almost lost beneath the chaos, but everything around it seems to move in time with the sound. Then you see it, standing still amid the battle, mouth moving in a rhythm that does not belong to breath.

## Combat Feel

Every word pushes back against thought. Spells hesitate. Movements feel out of sync. For a moment, a player is not sure the next action is theirs.

## Death Beat

The chant stops mid-syllable. Not cut off—just ended. The silence that follows feels heavier than the noise ever did.

## Core Mechanics

Frozen Voice Aura: concentration pressure nearby.

Dirge-Bound: undead resist fear while near Skald.

Resonance Clash: when a player casts a spell, Skald may contest with CHA. Let Performance/Persuasion be valid for player-facing identity.

Counter-Chant should be limited and dramatic. This is spotlight pressure, not shutdown.

## Bard Spotlight

If the Bard wins the contest, let the battlefield hesitate or the spell punch through resistance. Make it feel like a duel inside the battle.
