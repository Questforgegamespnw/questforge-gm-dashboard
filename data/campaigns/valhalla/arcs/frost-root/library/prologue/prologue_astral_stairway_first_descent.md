# Prologue — The Astral Stairway & First Descent

> Scene 0 is the runway into Act 1. It is not a detached arc.

## Purpose

Establish death, rebirth, multi-afterlife cosmology, Valhalla as a functional hub, the Einherjar deployment loop, and immediate transition into active conflict.

## Core Truth

Players should feel: **“We were chosen.”**

Hidden truth: **They were assigned to a failing system.**

## Phase 0 — Death & Dissolution

Players begin at legendary scale and describe heroic deaths. Each death should involve sacrifice, conviction, or a final choice. Combat is not required.

They awaken as shades in the Astral Stairway / The Way:

- forms indistinct
- memory suppressed
- thoughts unstable
- no sky, no ground, only direction

This suppression protects conscious souls from astral predators. Do not explain this fully. Let players feel disoriented, not confused.

### Boxed Text

There is no sky.  
No ground.  
Only… direction.  
Your form is incomplete—shadow without edge.  
Thoughts slip when you try to hold them.  
Names feel distant.  
Around you, others drift.  
Not walking.  
Not floating.  
Just… continuing.

## Phase 1 — The Shepherds

Figures appear and call to wandering souls. These are death shepherds tied to different afterlives. This reveals the sorting system without explaining it.

### Baldur

Line:

> “Those with gold or orange tickets—come with me.”

Tone: calm, certain, not explained.

Purpose:

- establish multi-afterlife support
- show souls are being routed
- seed later “on loan” logic
- preserve Loki / Balder suspicion for later, without confirming anything

## Phase 2 — Transition to Valhalla

The gray void snaps into full reality.

### Boxed Text

Color returns violently.  
Sky.  
Structure.  
Weight.  
The world doesn’t fade in—  
It asserts itself.

Purpose: Valhalla feels real, safe, and powerful.

## Phase 3 — Rebirth with Sven

Sven, keeper of the mead, receives the party with warmth and grounding. He gives them Yggdrasilmaed.

### Boxed Text

The drink burns—then settles.  
Your body remembers itself.  
Your thoughts… return.  
Names. Faces. Purpose.  
You are whole again.

### Yggdrasilmaed

Each player gains 4 uses. Per use:

- heal 50% HP; or
- restore spell slots / abilities as pseudo-long-rest without healing

This enables high-pressure encounters from session 1.

## Phase 4 — Svinhild and the Truth

The party meets Svinhild, a Valkyrie commander, while she is running aerial combat drills.

Reveal only what is needed:

- not all party members belong to Valhalla
- some are on loan from aligned divine domains
- enemies are attempting to force Ragnarok
- players are a specialized Einherjar strike unit
- operational secrecy matters

Strike loop:

1. Deploy — Bifrost Down
2. Assess
3. Neutralize
4. Extract — Valkyrie Up

## Heimdall Moment

Before deployment, Heimdall gives a short, ominous continuity line.

Use one:

- “You will be seen.”
- “You return.”
- “You return… mostly.”
- Silence.

Do not explain.

## Phase 5 — First Drop

### Boxed Text

Light tears open above you—  
You are pulled—  
Not falling—  
Delivered.  
The world slams into place beneath you.  
Snow erupts outward.  
And for the first time since death—  
You hear war.

Then immediately enter Act 1 Phase 0.

## Player Outlook Tracking

Track quietly:

- Trusting: accepts system; smooth mission flow
- Uncertain: asks questions; more perception hooks
- Suspicious: challenges framing; Heimdall lines hit harder

## Final Prologue Design Truth

This session teaches players:

- death is reversible
- Valhalla is safe
- the system works

So later, you can break all three.
