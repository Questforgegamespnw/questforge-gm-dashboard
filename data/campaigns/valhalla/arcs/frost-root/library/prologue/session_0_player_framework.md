# Session 0 Framework — Mythic Alignment

## Core Player-Facing Truth

> You are not becoming heroes. You already were.

## Starting Power

Characters begin at Level 10 as established legends, not beginners. Ask:

- What did I accomplish?
- Who knew my name?
- What was I known for?

If a concept feels like “Level 3 but stronger,” push it further.

## Legendary Equipment Buy-In

Each player should collaborate on one legendary identity item. It should reinforce playstyle, enable cool decisions, scale with Ragnarok pressure, and feel personal.

Prompt:

> What is the weapon, tool, or artifact you were known for?

## The Death Question

Each character must answer:

> How did you die?

This is not just backstory. It is the moment that made them worthy of Valhalla.

Encourage sacrifice, conviction, and final choice. Combat, glory, and recognition are optional.

Prompts:

- What did you refuse to let fall?
- Who lived because you didn’t?
- What did you give up knowing it would cost you everything?
- What belief did you refuse to abandon even when it killed you?
- Do you believe your death was enough?

## Optional Memory Fragments

Each player defines:

- one clear memory from death
- one unclear or distorted element

Use these later for Echo Self, Hel, Loki, Shadow Root, or identity pressure.

## Party Alignment Questions

- Did any of you know each other before death?
- Would your characters trust each other immediately?
- Who looks like a leader?
- Who looks like a problem?

## Tone Agreement

This campaign is mythic, emotional, and occasionally unsettling. Themes include death and return, identity and memory, duty vs choice, and system vs truth.

## Closing Line

> You all died. That part is done. Now the question is—what do you become when death stops being the end?
