# Resonance Clash System

## Purpose

Turn spellcasting into a duel of will.

This system spotlights the Bard and makes contested magic feel theatrical.

## Trigger

When:

- The Bard casts a spell
- A Skald reacts with Counter-Chant or similar contested song-magic

## Contest

Roll contested checks:

- Bard: CHA using Performance or Persuasion
- Skald: CHA

## Outcomes

### Bard Wins

Choose one:

- Spell enhanced
- Spell ignores resistance
- Spell gains a bonus effect

### Skald Wins

Choose one:

- Spell weakened
- Partial failure
- Spell effect reduced

## DM Lever

Use Resonance Clash to turn:

> “I cast a spell.”

into:

> “I challenge your magic.”

## Design Note

This should not happen every spell forever. Use it when the Skald is present, reactive, and important enough to contest the Bard’s moment.
