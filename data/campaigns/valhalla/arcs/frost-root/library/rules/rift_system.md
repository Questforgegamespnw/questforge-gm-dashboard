# Rift System

## Purpose

Rifts are an escalation engine.

A Rift is the DM’s chaos spike button.

## Effects

Rifts can:

- Spawn enemies
- Warp terrain
- Create urgency
- Distort space, timing, or mission conditions
- Trigger fear rerolls through corruption interference

## Player Options

Players may:

- Destroy the Rift
- Enter the Rift at high risk
- Suppress it through a skill challenge
- Ignore it and risk escalation

## DM Lever

Use a Rift when combat or mission pressure needs a spike.

Good Rift uses:

- Break a static battle line
- Force players to choose between objective and safety
- Introduce reinforcements
- Signal that the Root is actively destabilizing the zone
