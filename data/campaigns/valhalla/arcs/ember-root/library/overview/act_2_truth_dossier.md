# Act 2 Truth Dossier — The Ashen Root

> “Nothing here is broken. It is all working too well.”

## Master Truth

The Ashen Root is:

- Not corrupted.
- Not maliciously sentient.
- Not targeting anything.

It is amplifying all input beyond safe thresholds.

## What That Means

- Heat becomes inferno.
- Pressure becomes rupture.
- Energy becomes overload.
- Conflict becomes collapse.

## The Core

The Living Core is a regulator system.

It is designed to:

- Stabilize energy flow.
- Contain elemental force.

It is currently doing its job under impossible conditions.

The Core is not the villain.

The Core is not the cause.

## The Real Failure

The system failed because:

- Forge Lords increased demand and ignored warnings.
- Engineers knew something was wrong but could not agree.
- Workers bore the cost.

## Campaign-Level Truth

> The Root reveals what systems become under pressure.

## Player Perception Progression

### Early Act

Players may believe:

- “Something is wrong with the Root.”
- “The Core is unstable.”
- “We need to fix or destroy it.”

### Middle Act

Players begin to notice:

- Systems fail inconsistently.
- There is no single source of corruption.
- External enemies are not the root cause.

### End of Act

Players should realize:

- There is no clean solution.
- The system is functioning, just overloaded.
- Every solution creates loss.

## System Truths

### Root Truth

- Amplifies, does not corrupt.
- Escalates, does not direct.

### Core Truth

- Stabilizer under stress.
- Becomes dangerous only when destabilized or interfered with.

### Faction Truth

| Faction | Truth |
|---|---|
| Forge Lords | Caused overload. |
| Engineers | Understand the system. |
| Workers | Saw it coming. |
| Giants | Opportunists. |

### Time Truth

- Delay means escalation.
- Action means tradeoff.
- No neutral time exists.

## Player Misconception Tracker

| “The Core is the enemy.” | It is the only thing holding collapse back. |

## Foreshadowing Track for Act 3

Begin introducing subtle anomalies:

- Misremembered locations.
- Slight environmental shifts.
- NPC contradictions.

Do not explain yet.

## Valkyrie Strain

Show:

- Delayed responses.
- Hesitation.
- Missed souls.

This becomes critical later.

## Bifrost Instability

Use:

- Rough transitions.
- Flickering light.
- Uneven travel.

## DM Consistency Rules

Always remember:

- The system is not malicious.
- The Core is not evil.
- The Root is not corrupt.
- Problems escalate logically.
- Consequences follow choices.
- No outcome is clean.

## Final Master Truth

This act is not about stopping disaster.

It is about deciding what survives it.
