# EmberRoot Finale Game-Day Sheet — Controlled Release

## Starting State

The party stopped total CPP lockout in the throne chamber. Kaldra reached the dais, rolled a 31 with party support, and shut down the authority severance before it completed. She was then downed by two high-damage shots and stabilized.

CPP still ran for three rounds. Three of five main arteries were cut as damage control. Most shutoffs, safeties, and overflow meters are blown and unrecoverable.

The city is not saved. It is still reachable.

## GM Anchor

They stopped tyranny from completing its command. Now they have to save people from the machinery tyranny already broke.

## Three-Lane Operation

1. Core / Midworks Decommission — party and Kaldra use partial control to vent, redirect, cool, release, and seal.
2. Giant Line-Breaking — Skarth and giants break damaged lines only when named and timed.
3. Ashbound Evacuation — Bruni moves civilians through hidden routes before tunnels drop below the lava line.

## Timing Rhythm

Hold pressure. Cut the named line. Move civilians through the safe window.

Early cut: refugees are endangered.  
Late cut: pressure ruptures uncontrolled.  
Correct timing: decommission and evacuation both advance.

## Tunnel Risk

Track named routes simply:

- Stable
- Sagging
- Below / lava-filled

Use route drops when timing fails, Crown Anvil interferes, or pressure needs to spike.

## Durgrim / Crown Anvil

Durgrim escaped at 3 HP. He returns installed in the Crown Anvil / gilded submarine throne. The vessel attacks timing, routes, and the override window. Do not run it as a normal HP boss. Skarth and giants contest it while the party does the decisive work.

## Ending Target

Controlled release, reduced EmberRoot absorption, survivors choosing next steps without the old caste geometry.
