# Fire Giant Motivation

> “They don’t want to destroy the system. They want to free it.”

## Core Philosophy

Fire giants believe:

- Containment is weakness.
- Pressure is purpose.
- Power must be allowed to become what it is.

## What They See

Where dwarves see:

- Failure.
- Overload.
- Catastrophe.

Fire giants see:

- Transformation.

## Their Interpretation of the Ashen Root

The Root is not:

- A stabilizing force.
- A dangerous anomaly.

It is:

> A forge of reality itself.

## Their View of the Core

The Living Core is not a protector.

It is:

> A cage placed on something divine.

Their belief:

> “You built a god—and chained it.”

## Their End Goal

They are not trying to:

- Destroy Emberfall.
- Kill everyone.
- Win a battle.

They are trying to:

> Force the system into full release.

## Specific Objectives

### 1. Break Containment

- Destroy regulator pillars.
- Overload the Core.

### 2. Force Ascension Event

They believe that if fully released, the Core will:

- Evolve.
- Reshape the region.
- Create a new true forge.

### 3. Claim the Aftermath

After release:

- Giants thrive in extreme heat.
- Dwarven infrastructure collapses.
- Power shifts permanently.

## Campaign-Level Truth

The giants are not wrong.

But they are willing to pay a cost others are not.

## Thematic Parallel

| Group | Belief |
|---|---|
| Dwarves | Control the system |
| Ironhand Guilds | Fix the system |
| Ashbound | Escape the system |
| Giants | Become the system |

## How to Play Giants

Never play them as:

- Mindless brutes.
- Random attackers.

Always play them as:

- Calm under chaos.
- Impressed by destruction.
- Dismissive of fear.

## Core Giant Lines

- “You call this failure?”
- “This is becoming.”
- “You feel it, don’t you?”
- “Why would you stop this?”
