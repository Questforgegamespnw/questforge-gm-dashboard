# EmberRoot Consequence System

Post–Council of Embers Resolution Tables

## Core Rule

After the players make their choice, increase the Ragnarok Meter.

| Choice | Meter Increase |
|---|---:|
| Reinforce | +2 |
| Controlled Shutdown | +1 |
| Collapse | +2 |
| Release | +3 |

Even the best outcome still advances the end.

## Option 1 — Reinforce the System

> “Hold the line at any cost.”

Immediate outcome:

- City survives mostly
- Core remains active
- Fight is harder

### Reinforce Consequences — 1d6

| d6 | Result |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Structural Cracks: City holds, but fractures spread. Future missions here are more dangerous. |
| 2 | Worker Casualties: Ashbound losses are high. Bruni becomes hostile toward leadership and possibly the party. |
| 3 | Overloaded Constructs: Constructs begin malfunctioning intermittently. Future encounters include unstable constructs. |
| 4 | Valkyrie Delay: Extraction is slower than expected. First hint: “We couldn’t reach everyone in time.” |
| 5 | Hidden Instability: Core seems stable for now. Next fire-based arc gets bonus escalation. |
| 6 | Political Fallout: Forge Lords strengthen grip. City becomes more authoritarian. |

## Option 2 — Controlled Shutdown

> “Lose something now to save more later.”

Immediate outcome:

- Core weakened / stabilized
- City partially damaged
- Best balanced outcome

### Shutdown Consequences — 1d6

| d6 | Result |
|---:|---|
| 1 | District Lost: One major section destroyed. Survivors displaced. |
| 2 | Resource Collapse: Forge output drops dramatically. Weapons/gear become scarcer in future. |
| 3 | Valkyrie Strain: Extraction requires more effort. Valkyries visibly fatigued. |
| 4 | Engineer Divide: Kaldra gains influence. Internal faction tension increases. |
| 5 | Lingering Core Echo: Elemental instability remains. Occasional flare-ups. |
| 6 | “We Chose Who Lived”: Survivors question the party’s priorities. Emotional fallout. |

## Option 3 — Let It Collapse

> “Save the people, not the system.”

Immediate outcome:

- City heavily damaged or lost
- High survival among workers
- Infrastructure gone

### Collapse Consequences — 1d6

| d6 | Result |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Refugee Crisis: Survivors flood other regions. Future social complications. |
| 2 | Valkyrie Overload: Massive extraction effort. Valkyries begin showing real exhaustion. |
| 3 | Giant Expansion: Fire giants claim territory. New enemy stronghold established. |
| 4 | Core Residue: Fragment of Living Core survives. Potential future mini-boss. |
| 5 | Worker Loyalty: Bruni and Ashbound become strong allies. |
| 6 | Loss of Knowledge: Important schematics/tech destroyed. Future solutions harder. |

## Option 4 — Release the Core

> “Let it become what it was meant to be.”

Immediate outcome:

- Massive destruction
- Boss fight becomes chaotic and mythic
- Fire giants benefit

### Release Consequences — 1d6

| d6 | Result |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Elemental Rift: Fire Plane influence leaks into world. Future missions include planar bleed. |
| 2 | Giant Ascendancy: Skarth gains power and becomes recurring villain. |
| 3 | Valkyrie Failure: Multiple souls not retrieved. Quiet Rows destabilizes. |
| 4 | Core Fragment Awakens: A piece of the entity survives independently. Future boss hook. |
| 5 | Widespread Destruction: Entire region uninhabitable. Map permanently altered. |
| 6 | “You Chose This”: NPCs directly blame the party. Reputation impact across realms. |

## Universal Consequence Table — 1d6

Roll once regardless of choice.

| d6 | Result |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Heimdall Notice: “You chose… quickly.” |
| 2 | Valkyrie Conflict: Two Valkyries argue openly. |
| 3 | Quiet Rows Flicker: A name almost disappears. |
| 4 | Mead Tastes Different: Subtle but noticeable. |
| 5 | Returned Einherjar Hesitates: Small behavioral glitch. |
| 6 | Bifrost Distortion: Arrival/return feels unstable. |

## Design Philosophy

Every choice:

- Solves one problem
- Creates another
- Advances Ragnarok

## Player Feedback Tool

Sven can say:

> “You did what you could.”
>
> “…it’s just getting harder, isn’t it?”

## Campaign Impact

After this arc:

- World feels less stable
- Valkyries are more strained
- Players understand there are no clean victories anymore
