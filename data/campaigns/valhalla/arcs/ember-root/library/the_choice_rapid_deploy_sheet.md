# The Choice — GM Rapid Deploy Sheet

> “Pick fast. Run faster.”

## Step 1 — Set State

Note:

- Insight: Low / Mid / High
- Core: Unstable / Mixed / Stabilized
- Clock: 0–2 / 3–4 / 5–6

## Step 2 — Opening Line

### Unstable

> “The chamber is breaking. Not straining—breaking.”

### Mixed

> “The chamber shudders in uneven bursts—like something below is deciding when to hold.”

### Stabilized

> “The chamber holds… but only just.”

## Step 3 — Tri-Voice Burst

Forge Lord:

> “Hold the system!”

Engineer:

> “That will break it!”

Worker:

> “We’re not dying for it!”

Then look at the players.

## Step 4 — Present Options

Do not overexplain.

### Reinforce

> “Hold it together.”

### Shutdown

> “Reduce the load.”

### Collapse

> “Save the people.”

### Release

> “Let it become something else.”

If High Insight, add:

> “You understand what each of these will cost.”

## Step 5 — Force Decision

If hesitation:

### Clock 5–6

> “You don’t have time.”

### Clock 3–4

> “You have seconds—not minutes.”

### Clock 0–2

> “Whatever you choose—it happens now.”

## Step 6 — Apply State Modifier

High Insight Bonus, choose one:

- Save one extra crisis later.
- Reduce one consequence.
- Safer exfil.

Core State Effect:

| Core | Effect |
|---|---|
| Unstable | Outcome worsens |
| Mixed | Standard |
| Stabilized | Outcome improves |

## Step 7 — Hard Cut to Collapse

Read:

> “The moment you decide—the system reacts.”
