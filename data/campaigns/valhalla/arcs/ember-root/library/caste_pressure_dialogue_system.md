# Caste Pressure Dialogue System

> “Pressure Through People”

## Purpose

Act 2 is too chaotic for long conversations, so the caste system should come through as pressure bursts, not speeches.

This system injects caste conflict into active scenes without slowing play.

It works during:

- Combat
- Movement
- Crisis
- Decision moments

## Tension Triangle

Always active in scenes:

| Group | Pressure |
|---|---|
| Forge Lords | Control / Endure |
| Engineers | Fix / Stabilize |
| Workers | Escape / Survive |

Players are always between these three forces.

## Core Tool — Tri-Voice Burst

At any moment of tension:

1. Drop three rapid-fire lines, one per caste.
2. Immediately return control to the players.

Use when:

- Players hesitate.
- A new crisis appears.
- A system escalates.
- A choice is about to happen.

## Base Tri-Voice Template

Forge Lord:

> “Hold the system!”

Engineer:

> “That will overload it!”

Worker:

> “We’re not dying for this!”

Then look at players.

## Variant Bursts by Context

### Investigation — System Thread

Engineer:

> “It’s exceeding safe thresholds!”

Forge Lord:

> “Then increase capacity!”

Worker:

> “We’re the ones inside that capacity!”

### Investigation — Giant Thread

Forge Lord:

> “Defend the perimeter!”

Engineer:

> “They’re not the priority!”

Worker:

> “Tell that to the ones getting crushed!”

### Investigation — Root Thread

Engineer:

> “This is deeper than the city!”

Forge Lord:

> “Then we contain it here!”

Worker:

> “You can’t contain this!”

## Combat Injection Lines

### Chaotic Combat

Worker:

> “We’re still down here!”

Engineer:

> “Stabilize that line or we lose everything!”

Forge Lord:

> “Maintain formation!”

### System Failure Moment

Engineer:

> “That’s not supposed to happen!”

Worker:

> “It’s been happening all day!”

Forge Lord:

> “Adapt!”

## Unsavable Injection Set

### Civilian Crisis

Worker:

> “Please—over here!”

Forge Lord:

> “Hold your position!”

Engineer:

> “That structure won’t last!”

### Collapse Event

Engineer:

> “The supports are failing!”

Forge Lord:

> “Reinforce them!”

Worker:

> “With what?”

### Lava Surge

Worker:

> “It’s coming through!”

Engineer:

> “That channel’s blown!”

Forge Lord:

> “Redirect it!”

## Council Pressure Bursts

### Escalation Moment

Forge Lord:

> “We endure!”

Engineer:

> “We adapt!”

Worker:

> “We survive!”

### Decision Moment

Engineer:

> “You know what this will cost!”

Worker:

> “Then don’t choose them!”

Forge Lord:

> “Choose the city!”

## Micro-Dialogue System

NPC lines should be:

- One sentence.
- Emotionally charged.
- Interruptible.

Examples:

Kaldra:

> “Too much input—nowhere for it to go!”

Bruni:

> “We’re done listening!”

Durgrim:

> “Hold the line!”

Skarth:

> “You feel it too, don’t you?”

## Pressure Escalation Ladder

| Pressure | Dialogue Mode |
|---|---|
| Low | Debate / explanation |
| Mid | Interruptions / arguments |
| High | Shouting / commands / desperation |

Act 2 should mostly live in Mid to High.

## Hub Carryover

Hub means quiet reflection.

Field means loud pressure.

Post-act lines:

Worker survivor:

> “They said it would hold…”

Engineer:

> “We were close…”

Valkyrie:

> “…not close enough.”

## Quick-Use Cheat Tool

If you need a line fast:

Forge Lord:

> “Hold it!”

Engineer:

> “That’s wrong!”

Worker:

> “We’re leaving!”

## Player Alignment Impact

If players side with Forge Lords:

- Gain authority.
- Lose worker trust.

If players side with Engineers:

- Gain information.
- Create tension.

If players side with Workers:

- Gain loyalty.
- Lose system control.

## Post-Choice World State

| Decision | Caste Impact |
|---|---|
| Reinforce | Lords empowered; workers oppressed |
| Shutdown | Engineers rise; system weakened |
| Collapse | Workers empowered; city lost |
| Release | Giants gain power; all castes destabilized |

## Final Caste Truth

The problem was never just the system.

It was who controlled it, who maintained it, and who suffered under it.
