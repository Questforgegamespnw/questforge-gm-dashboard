# Micro Encounter Modules

## High Forge — Control vs Denial

### Module 1 — Hold the Line
Guards block a passage downward. Workers try to evacuate. Engineers argue to reroute flow. Truth: the order is making things worse.

Outcomes: let workers pass reduces future civilian casualties; hold line increases Unsavable severity; force through creates authority backlash.

### Module 2 — The Control Room Lie
Engineers insist stabilization is under control, but readings spike inconsistently. Truth: they force a narrative of control.

Outcomes: call it out gains Kaldra alignment; accept it delays realization and adds Clock +1.

## Midworks — System vs Overload

### Module 3 — Argument at the Node
Two engineer teams disagree: reduce input vs increase capacity. Worker: “You’re both killing us!” Truth: both are partially right and wrong.

Outcomes: correct interpretation gives Insight bonus; wrong choice worsens local system; delay adds Clock +1.

### Module 4 — Overload Cascade
One system failure triggers another. Players choose one: stabilize node A, redirect flow B, evacuate workers C. Truth: cannot stop the cascade fully.

Outcome: stabilize one, others fail.

## Chainways — Movement vs Collapse

### Module 7 — The Blocked Crossing
A chain bridge is overcrowded and collapsing. Clear path, reinforce, or redirect. Truth: any solution sacrifices someone’s path.

### Module 8 — The Fall
A platform gives way mid-crossing. Split-second reactions and skill saves. Truth: you cannot catch everyone.
