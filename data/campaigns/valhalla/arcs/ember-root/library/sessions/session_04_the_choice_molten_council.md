# Session 4 — The Choice / Molten Council

> “You know enough to choose. Now choose.”

## Scene Purpose
Make consequence explicit, reflect investigation path, present Insight without resolving consequence, lock in the final decision, and set up Hot Exfil / The Unsavable.

## Council State Inputs
- Insight Level: Low = fear/confusion, Mid = partial understanding, High = accurate understanding.
- Core State: Stable = debate, Mixed = urgent argument, Unstable = panic.
- Clock Pressure: 0–2 heated debate, 3–4 urgent argument, 5–6 forced decision.

## Player Authority
Do not ask “What do you want to do?” Ask: “You are the only ones who understand enough to choose.”

## Options
- Reinforce: hold it together.
- Shutdown: reduce load.
- Collapse: save people first.
- Release: let power grow.

## Decision Lock
Once players choose: no backsies, no debate reset, no perfect clarification.

## Join Skarth Branch
Only offer if players reached High Insight, expressed doubt about control or sympathy toward Skarth, and destabilized/questioned stabilization. Do not offer if they are clearly in heroic save-everyone mode.

Skarth: “You already see it. This doesn’t need to be saved. It needs to be allowed. Help me.”

Safety line if unsure: “This will make things worse for the city.”

### If Players Help Skarth
Forge Below objective flips from stabilize system to break containment faster.

New actions: destroy pillar, disrupt stabilization, protect giant strike team.

Benefits: Core easier to interact with, Giants assist, faster progression.

Costs: Stability plummets, future crises worsen, civilian losses increase.

## Unsavable Variant
+1 additional crisis: Runaway Transformation. Environment mutates; fire behaves unnaturally.

## Hot Exfil Variant
Not escape from collapse, but escape from uncontrolled transformation. Valkyrie reaction: “What have you done?”

## Skarth Council Reframe
When accused, Skarth does not deny. He says: “Yes. And?”

### Unstable Core
“You blame us… for what your system has already become? We didn’t break it. We stopped pretending it wasn’t broken.”

### Mixed Core
“You think we caused this? No… we simply refused to stop it. You had the same choice. They still do.”

### Stable Core
“You call this stability? You’re suffocating it. Holding something like this in chains… is the real crime.”

### High Insight Address
“You’ve seen it. You know this isn’t failure. Why are you helping them lie about it?”

## Tri-Voice Collision
Forge Lord: “This is your doing!” Engineer: “That’s not entirely—” Worker: “We’re the ones dying!” Skarth: “And still… you argue about blame.”

## Final Design Truth
Skarth is not arguing “We are right.” He is arguing “You already know we’re right.”
