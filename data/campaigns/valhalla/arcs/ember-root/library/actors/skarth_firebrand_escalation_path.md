# Skarth Firebrand — Escalation Path

> “Let it burn free.”

## Design Role

Skarth is not just an enemy.

He is the voice of a different answer to the act's core question.

## Escalation Structure

### Stage 1 — Observer

Investigation phase.

Role:

- Present but not aggressive.
- Tests players.

Behavior:

- Calm.
- Curious.
- Slightly amused.

Lines:

- “You’re trying to fix it.”
- “Why?”

### Stage 2 — Influencer

Mid-act.

Role:

- Begins interfering.
- Pushes toward escalation.

Actions:

- Sends strike teams.
- Creates pressure events.

Lines:

- “You’re holding it back.”
- “You could let it become more.”

### Stage 3 — Agent of Release

Forge Below / Council.

Role:

- Direct opposition.
- Attempts to trigger full release.

Triggers:

- Players stabilize the system.
- Players prevent collapse.

Actions:

- Attacks pillars.
- Forces instability spikes.

### Stage 4 — Boss Escalation

Optional / conditional.

Trigger if:

- Players strongly oppose him.
- Players ignore/delay threat.
- Core/unstable escalation occurs.

## Ascendant Form

Skarth’s ascendant form should feel like the environment is feeding him.

## Quick Boss Frame

Use only if the story demands it.

Suggested frame:

- AC: 18.
- HP: 180–220.
- Speed: 40 ft.

## Core Traits

### Embrace the Flame

Gains power as the environment worsens.

### Rupture Pulse

Recharge ability.

Triggers:

- Lava surge.
- Structural collapse.
- Core flare.

### Break the Chains

Targets containment systems.

## Fight Goal

Skarth is not trying to kill players.

He is trying to:

> Force the system into catastrophic release.

## Unique Mechanic — Environment Feeds Him

| Event | Effect |
|---|---|
| Lava spreads | He heals |
| Core flares | He gains damage |
| Structure collapse | He gains modifier |

## Dialogue During Fight

- “You still try to control it.”
- “Let it go.”
- “This is what it was meant to be.”

## Important Design Rule

Players can:

- Fight him.
- Delay him.
- Drive him off.

But:

- Killing him is not required.
- He may escape.

## Post-Act Impact

### If Skarth Succeeds Partially

- Future fire zones intensify.
- Elemental bleed increases.

### If Skarth Fails

- He becomes a recurring antagonist.
- More personal vendetta.

## Final Truth

Skarth represents the one choice the players are afraid to take:

- Not control.
- Not survival.
- Transformation through destruction.
