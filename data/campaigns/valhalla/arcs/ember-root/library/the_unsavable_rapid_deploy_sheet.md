# The Unsavable — GM Rapid Deploy Sheet

> “You can’t save all of it.”

## Step 1 — Set Crisis Count

| Clock | Crises |
|---|---:|
| 0–2 | 3 |
| 3–4 | 4 |
| 5–6 | 5 |

## Step 2 — Select Crises

Pick from:

- Civilians: people trapped / pinned.
- Structure: bridge / platform collapse.
- Lava: flow breach / spreading hazard.
- Enemies: giants / constructs pushing in.
- Core Flare: energy surge / instability spike.

## Step 3 — Opening

Read fast:

> “It all happens at once.”

## Step 4 — Tri-Voice Burst

Worker:

> “Help us!”

Engineer:

> “That structure won’t hold!”

Forge Lord:

> “Hold your position!”

## Step 5 — Player Choice

Rule:

Players fully resolve:

- 1 crisis.
- 2 crises if High Insight.

Say:

> “Pick one.”

## Step 6 — Resolve Chosen Crisis

| Crisis | Success | Cost Elsewhere |
|---|---|---|
| Civilians | Survivors saved | Others die elsewhere |
| Structure | Path secured | Other routes collapse |
| Lava | Flow contained | Fire spreads elsewhere |
| Enemies | Pressure reduced | Collapse worsens |
| Core | Stabilization | System shifts unpredictably |

## Step 7 — Auto-Fail the Rest

Everything else resolves without players.

Read:

> “You hear it before you see it—something else fails.”

## Step 8 — Escalate Based on Core State

| Core State | Escalation |
|---|---|
| Unstable | Add extra hazard; immediate collapse event |
| Mixed | Random failure; split pressure |
| Stabilized | Delayed failure; more controlled |

## Step 9 — Hot Exfil Trigger

Read:

> “The ground gives way.”

## Exfil Quick Rules

| State | Exfil |
|---|---|
| Stable | Clear path; few hazards |
| Mixed | Changing routes |
| Unstable | Constant hazards; path collapse |

Failures may include:

- Damage.
- NPC loss.
- Separation.

## Final Button

End with:

> “You saved who you could.”
>
> “But not all of them.”

## Zero-Hesitation Rules

Never:

- Explain too much.
- Pause pacing.
- Offer perfect options.

Always:

- Force decisions quickly.
- Cut to consequence immediately.
- Keep momentum.
