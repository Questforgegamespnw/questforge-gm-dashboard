# Investigation Clock & Insight System

## Investigation Clock

### Purpose

Tracks:

- Time spent.
- Delay.
- Indecision.

### How It Increases

Increase by 1 when:

- A thread is explored.
- Players hesitate or debate too long.
- Players rest or delay.

### Breakpoints

| Clock | Effect |
|---:|---|
| 2 | Minor instability |
| 4 | Major system failure |
| 6 | Critical cascade |

### DM Rule

When unsure, increase the clock.

## Insight System

### Purpose

Tracks player understanding of the truth.

### Levels

- Low
- Mid
- High

### High Insight Unlocks

- Better Council clarity.
- Extra crisis resolution.
- Safer Exfil.
- Join Skarth option.

## Design Note

Clock is pressure.

Insight is comprehension.

Together they determine how hard the choice lands and how much mitigation the players have earned.
