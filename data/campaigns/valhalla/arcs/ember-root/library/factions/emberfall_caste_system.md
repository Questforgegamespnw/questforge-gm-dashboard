# The Emberfall Caste System

> “The forge does not fail. The people inside it do.”

## Design Purpose

This system must:

- Reinforce the theme of systemic failure.
- Create background tension in every scene.
- Provide crowd behavior.
- Provide social conflict.
- Provide moral weight.
- Make players feel that this problem existed before they arrived.

## The Three Castes

- Forge Lords.
- Ironhand Guilds.
- Ashbound.

## Forge Lords — Upper Caste

> “The system must endure.”

### Role

- Governance.
- Production control.
- Core oversight.

### Belief

- Stability is more important than people.
- Output equals survival.
- Weakness invites collapse.

### Truth

They:

- Caused overload.
- Ignored warnings.
- Still believe they are right.

### Crowd Behavior

- Controlled.
- Defensive.
- Dismissive of panic.

### Generic Lines

- “Hold the line.”
- “This is contained.”
- “We endure.”

### In Play

Use them to:

- Resist player suggestions.
- Support Durgrim.
- Justify bad decisions.

## Ironhand Guilds — Middle Caste

> “We built this. We understand it.”

### Role

- Engineers.
- Rune specialists.
- System maintainers.

### Belief

- Problems are solvable.
- The system can be corrected.
- Logic should prevail.

### Truth

They:

- Knew the risks.
- Were divided.
- Failed to act decisively.

### Crowd Behavior

- Arguing constantly.
- Running calculations mid-crisis.
- Split into factions.

### Generic Lines

- “That’s not the issue!”
- “You’re misreading the flow!”
- “We can still stabilize it!”

### In Play

Use them to:

- Provide conflicting information.
- Feed investigation threads.
- Support Kaldra or oppose her.

## Ashbound — Lower Caste

> “We kept it running. They broke it.”

### Role

- Laborers.
- Furnace workers.
- Maintenance crews.

### Belief

- The system is killing them.
- Leadership failed.
- Survival is more important than structure.

### Truth

They:

- Were right.
- Were ignored.
- Are now done trusting authority.

### Crowd Behavior

- Panicked or defiant.
- Helping each other.
- Resisting orders.

### Generic Lines

- “We told you!”
- “Not this time!”
- “We’re getting out!”

### In Play

Use them to:

- Drive emotional stakes.
- Push players toward saving people.
- Reinforce Bruni’s position.

## Functional Summary

| Group | Pressure |
|---|---|
| Forge Lords | Control the system |
| Ironhand Guilds | Fix the system |
| Ashbound | Survive the system |
| Fire Giants | Become the system |
