# The Chainways

> “Everything connects. Everything can fail.”

## Overview

The connective tissue of Emberfall.

Purpose:

- Links all major districts.
- Critical for movement.
- Critical for evacuation.
- Critical for crisis escalation.

## Visual Identity

- Hanging chains.
- Swaying platforms.
- Open vertical space.
- Lava far below.

## DM Thematic Notes

This is where decisions become physical.

Players should feel:

- Exposure.
- Risk.
- Urgency.

## Playable Layout

### Main Chain Bridges

- Wide enough for groups.
- Collapse points.

### Secondary Paths

- Narrow.
- Faster routes.

### Vertical Drops

- Risk / reward movement.

## Caste Presence

Mixed.

All castes intersect here.

## Caste Behavior

Everyone collides here:

- Orders.
- Arguments.
- Panic.

## Injection Dialogue

Forge Lord:

> “Hold the line!”

Engineer:

> “This path won’t hold!”

Worker:

> “Move or die!”

## Final Design Note

Each district now has:

- A system.
- A caste.
- A theme.
- Dialogue that reinforces pressure, perspective, and conflict.
- Layouts that support session mechanics and crisis systems.

## What This Unlocks

You can now:

- Drop encounters anywhere.
- Tie every crisis to meaning.
- Reinforce theme without stopping play.
