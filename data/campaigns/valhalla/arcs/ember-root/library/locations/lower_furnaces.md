# The Lower Furnaces

> “This is where the cost lives.”

## Overview

The labor core of Emberfall.

Workers live and operate here.

Closest to:

- Heat source.
- Root resonance.

## Visual Identity

- Low visibility.
- Thick smoke.
- Extreme heat.
- Constant noise.

## DM Thematic Notes

This is where the truth was always visible.

Players should feel:

- Discomfort.
- Moral weight.
- “This was always wrong.”

## Playable Layout

### Furnace Pits

- Massive open heat zones.
- Hazard-heavy.

### Worker Quarters

- Cramped.
- Lived-in.
- Humanizing space.

### Resonance Tunnels

- Lead toward Root.
- Subtle energy pulses.

## Caste Presence

Dominant:

- Workers / Ashbound.

Secondary:

- Mining engineers.

## Caste Behavior

Workers:

- Angry.
- Tired.
- Done trusting leadership.

## Injection Dialogue

Worker:

> “We told them.”

Another:

> “They didn’t listen.”

Third, to players:

> “Are you going to?”
