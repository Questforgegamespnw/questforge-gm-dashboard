# The High Forge

> “Control is maintained... until it isn’t.”

## Overview

The administrative and command heart of Emberfall.

Houses:

- Council Chamber.
- Forge Lord command structures.

Physical role:

- Elevated above most of the city.
- Last zone to destabilize.

## Visual Identity

- Clean stone architecture compared to the rest of the city.
- Controlled lava channels.
- Heat distortion visible but managed.
- Guards and structured movement.

## DM Thematic Notes

This is the illusion of control zone.

Players should feel:

- “This place still works.”
- “Someone is in charge here.”

But show:

- Cracks forming.
- Tension in every interaction.

## Playable Layout

### Council Chamber

Core scene space.

- Circular.
- Lava-lit floor cracks.
- Central speaking platform.

### Command Walkways

- Overlook lower districts.
- Good for exposition.
- Good for observing collapse.

### Reinforcement Stations

- Guards attempting to hold structure.
- Can become crisis points.

## Caste Presence

Dominant:

- Forge Lords.

Secondary:

- Engineers / advisors.

## Caste Behavior

Forge Lords:

- Issuing orders.
- Dismissing panic.

Engineers:

- Arguing quietly.
- Presenting data no one wants.

## Injection Dialogue

Forge Lord:

> “The system holds.”

Engineer:

> “…not like this.”

Guard:

> “We’ve lost contact with the lower tiers!”
