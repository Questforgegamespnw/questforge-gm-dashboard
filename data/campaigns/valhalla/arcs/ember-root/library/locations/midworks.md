# The Midworks

> “This is where the system starts to show strain.”

## Overview

The operational heart of Emberfall.

Houses:

- Rune systems.
- Control mechanisms.
- Maintenance infrastructure.

## Visual Identity

- Constant sparks.
- Flickering rune arrays.
- Pipes venting heat unpredictably.
- Noise, motion, instability.

## DM Thematic Notes

This is where truth starts leaking through the system.

Players should realize:

- Failures are inconsistent.
- No single cause exists.

## Playable Layout

### Control Rooms

- Rune panels.
- Interactive systems.

### Maintenance Corridors

- Tight spaces.
- Heat bursts.

### Overload Nodes

- Mini-encounters.
- System failures.

## Caste Presence

Dominant:

- Engineers / Ironhand Guilds.

Secondary:

- Workers / Ashbound.

## Caste Behavior

Engineers:

- Arguing constantly.
- Trying to fix everything.

Workers:

- Frustrated.
- Being pushed too far.

## Injection Dialogue

Engineer:

> “That’s not the failure point!”

Another Engineer:

> “Yes it is!”

Worker:

> “While you argue—we’re burning!”
