# Dwarven Forces — Emberfall

## Design Principle

Dwarves are not enemies.

But they can become obstacles.

## Forge Guard

- Crowd control.
- Authority enforcement.
- Used in High Forge conflict and blocked evacuation.

## Ironhand Engineer

- Support / hazard interaction.
- Can help or hinder.
- Used in Midworks and system events.

## Ashbound Defender

- Civilian protector.
- Emotional combat.
- Used in Lower Furnaces and civilian crises.

## Table Use

Use dwarven forces when you need:

- Authority conflict.
- Rescue conflict.
- System interference.
- Caste pressure made physical.
