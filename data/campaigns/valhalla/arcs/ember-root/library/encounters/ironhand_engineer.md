# Ironhand Engineer

## Role

- Support / hazard interaction.
- Can help or hinder.

## Quick Stats

- AC: 14.
- HP: ~50.

## Traits

### Emergency Override

1/encounter.

Can stabilize or worsen the system.

## Use

- Midworks encounters.
- System events.

## Design Note

Ironhand Engineers should produce useful but conflicting pressure.

Their actions are often correct in theory and dangerous in context.
