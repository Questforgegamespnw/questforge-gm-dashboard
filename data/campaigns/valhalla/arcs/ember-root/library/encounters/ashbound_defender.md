# Ashbound Defender

> “Protect our own.”

## Role

- Civilian protector.
- Emotional combat.

## Quick Stats

- AC: 13.
- HP: ~55.

## Traits

### Desperation Strike

Bonus damage when protecting others.

## Use

- Lower Furnaces.
- Civilian crises.

## Design Note

Ashbound Defenders make violence emotionally complicated.

They are protecting people, not trying to win a battle.
