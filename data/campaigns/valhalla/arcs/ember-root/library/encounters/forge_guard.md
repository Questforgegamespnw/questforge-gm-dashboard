# Forge Guard

> “Maintain order.”

## Role

- Crowd control.
- Authority enforcement.

## Quick Stats

- AC: 17.
- HP: ~60.

## Traits

### Formation Defense

Gains bonus when near allies.

## Actions

### Warhammer

Standard melee.

## Use

- High Forge conflict.
- Blocking evacuation.

## Design Note

Forge Guards are not villains by default.

They become obstacles when order conflicts with survival.
