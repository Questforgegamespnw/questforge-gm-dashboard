# Ember Guard

> “Hold the advance.”

## Role

- Area denial.
- Protects other giants.

## Quick Stats

- AC: 18.
- HP: ~130.

## Traits

### Molten Guard

Reduces damage for allies behind it.

## Actions

### Shield Slam

- Push + knockback.

## Use

- Enemy Push crisis.
- Blocking escape routes.

## Design Note

The Ember Guard blocks choices.

Use it to make rescue, retreat, or system access harder.
