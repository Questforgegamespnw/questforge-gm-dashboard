# Magma Reaver

> “Break the system open.”

## Role

- Frontline disruptor.
- Structural breaker.

## Quick Stats

- AC: 17.
- HP: ~110.
- Speed: 30 ft.

## Traits

### Magma Body

Creatures that hit the Reaver in melee take minor fire damage.

### Shatter Ground

Recharge 5–6.

- Slam ground.
- Creates difficult terrain.
- Forces Dexterity save or target falls prone.

## Actions

### Multiattack

- 2 slam attacks.
- ~14–16 damage each.

## Use

- Structural collapse crisis.
- Chainway fights.
- Breaking routes.

## Design Note

The Reaver is a crisis amplifier.

It should break terrain, worsen routes, and force the party to choose between stopping it and handling the bigger disaster.
