# Cinder Channeler

> “Amplify the chaos.”

## Role

- Hazard amplifier.
- Core interaction synergy.

## Quick Stats

- AC: 15.
- HP: ~90.

## Traits

### Amplify Surge

Any lava or Core effect increases damage.

## Actions

### Lava Lash

- Ranged attack.
- Pulls target slightly.

### Surge Pulse

Recharge 5–6.

- Boosts environmental hazards for 1 round.

## Use

- Core Flare crisis.
- Lava breach escalation.
- Forge Below synergy.

## Design Note

The Channeler is a support/control enemy.

Its job is to make the battlefield worse, not to anchor a long fight.
