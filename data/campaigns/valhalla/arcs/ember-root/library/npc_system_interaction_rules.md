# NPC System Interaction Rules

## Purpose

NPCs in Act 2 are not simply information dispensers.

They are pressure systems.

They react to:

- Player insight.
- Core Stability.
- The Investigation Clock.
- Player priorities.
- The current caste conflict.

## System Modifiers

### If Players Have High Insight

NPCs:

- Trust them more.
- Defer to them more.
- Become more willing to follow risky but informed plans.

### If the Core Is Unstable

NPCs:

- Interrupt more.
- Panic faster.
- Argue emotionally.
- Stop speaking in clean explanations.

### If the Clock Is High

NPCs:

- Stop debating.
- Demand decisions.
- Push action over process.

## DM Rules for NPC Play

### Do

- Interrupt players occasionally.
- Let NPCs disagree.
- Show emotional escalation.

### Don't

- Let NPCs agree too easily.
- Provide clear answers.
- Resolve tension early.

## Final NPC Truth

Each NPC is not trying to be right.

They are trying to survive in the way they believe is correct.

## Core NPC Template

Each major NPC has:

1. Base identity.
2. Crisis philosophy.
3. State behavior.
4. Player response.
5. Post-choice evolution.
