# Crisis 2 — Structural Collapse

> “The path won’t hold.”

## Drop-In Open

> “The bridge is already breaking.”

## Situation

A critical route is failing.

People are still on it.

Others need it to escape.

The structure is shaking apart faster than anyone expected.

## Mechanics

Choose one style.

### Option A — Split Decision

Players choose one:

#### Reinforce the Structure

Preserves the route, but takes time.

#### Clear the People

Saves those present, but the route may be lost.

#### Abandon the Route

Fastest option, but guarantees future movement problems.

### Option B — Timed Challenge

Players have 2 rounds to act.

Each round worsens the collapse.

After the timer ends, the structure fails unless sufficiently stabilized.

## Outcomes

### Success

- Route preserved.
- Movement advantage during Hot Exfil.
- Valkyries have a safer landing / retrieval path.

### Partial

- Some people cross safely.
- Route becomes unstable.
- Hot Exfil gains a minor collapse hazard.

### Failure

- Path destroyed.
- People may fall or become trapped.
- Hot Exfil gains **Falling World** hazard: collapsing pillars, buildings, and platforms make landing / retrieval difficult.

## Core State Variants

### Unstable

Immediate collapse.

No full success possible; only partial rescue or controlled loss.

### Mixed

Collapse staggers in sections.

Players can save people or preserve part of the route.

### Stabilized

Full reinforcement is possible.

Success creates a reliable escape route later.
