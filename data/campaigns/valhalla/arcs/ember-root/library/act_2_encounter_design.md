# Act 2 Encounter Design

## Combat Philosophy

Act 2 is not combat-forward, but when fights happen they should feel:

- Fast.
- Purposeful.
- Tied to the system.
- Crisis-integrated.
- Not grindy.

## Rule 1 — Keep It Short

Use 1–3 rounds maximum.

The goal of combat is usually to:

- Disrupt.
- Escape.
- Protect.
- Delay.
- Force a choice.

## Rule 2 — Tie Combat to Crisis

Combat should always be part of a bigger problem.

Do not run enemies as isolated bags of hit points.

Attach every fight to:

- A hazard.
- A location state.
- A crisis.
- An evacuation route.
- A system failure.
- A decision timer.

## Rule 3 — No Full Clears

Enemies may:

- Retreat.
- Reposition.
- Escalate.
- Break something.
- Threaten another objective.

A successful encounter does not need to mean every enemy is dead.

## Quick Encounter Build Tool

Need a fight fast?

Pick:

1. One enemy type.
2. One hazard.
3. One objective.

Example:

- Magma Reaver.
- Collapsing bridge.
- Save civilians.

Done. Run it.

## Enemy Design Roles

### Fire Giants

Pressure, not boss fights.

They are:

- Disruptors.
- Hazard amplifiers.
- Area denial tools.
- System-release agents.

### Dwarven Forces

Obstacles, not villains.

They are:

- Enforcers.
- Protectors.
- Panicked defenders.
- System operators.
- People acting under caste pressure.
