## Common Player Questions / Answer Bank

Use Sylva when the players need the Valkyrie system to still feel noble, hopeful, and worth saving.

### Who are you?

“Sylva Emberwing.”

She brightens as if introductions are a kind of promise.

“I retrieve the worthy fallen. Or I will. More and more of them.”

**GM delivery note:** She is earnest, not naive in an annoying way. Her faith should be appealing.

### What do you actually do?

“I help bring people home.”

She says it simply.

“Not just back. Home. There is a difference.”

**GM delivery note:** Sylva makes the system sound beautiful.

### Why do you believe so strongly?

“Because I have seen what it does to someone when they realize death was not the end of their usefulness.”

She smiles.

“That moment matters.”

**GM delivery note:** Her belief has a real foundation. It is not empty propaganda.

### Can everyone be brought back?

“Yes.”

Too fast.

Then she steadies herself.

“That is what we are here for.”

**GM delivery note:** Let the too-fast answer reveal that this belief is emotionally necessary to her.

### What if someone cannot be brought back?

“That means we were not done trying.”

If pushed hard:

“I do not know what I would call it if we were.”

**GM delivery note:** This is fragile faith under pressure.

### Are you afraid?

“Of course.”

She lifts her chin.

“Courage without fear is just momentum.”

If asked what scares her:

“That someone will need us, and belief will not be enough.”

**GM delivery note:** This is a good bridge from bright to vulnerable.

### What is going wrong?

“I do not know.”

She hates saying it.

“I know people are tired. I know some briefings are shorter. I know Eldri looks sad after returns that should have been simple.”

**GM delivery note:** Sylva knows emotional symptoms, not root causes.

### Do you trust command?

“Yes.”

A beat.

“I think I have to.”

**GM delivery note:** Her trust is sincere but dependent on worldview stability.

### Do you trust us?

“I want to.”

If the players have acted honorably:

“You keep coming back and still asking what happens to others. That matters.”

**GM delivery note:** Sylva is a natural player ally if treated kindly.

### What should we do next?

“Help someone who still believes help is coming.”

If mission choice is pending:

“Choose the place where hope would do the most good.”

**GM delivery note:** Her mission advice is moral, not tactical.

### What do you think of the other Valkyries?

- **Svinhild:** “She makes hard things possible.”
- **Ryndis:** “She pretends not to care so people do not see how much she remembers.”
- **Eldri:** “The gentlest of us, and maybe the strongest.”
- **Thora:** “I wish I was that fearless. I know she is not.”
- **Kara:** “I do not think she dislikes me. I think she dislikes certainty.”
