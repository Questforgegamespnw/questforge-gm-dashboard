# The Forge-Lanes

> “War is not just fought. It is made.”

## Overview

The Forge-Lanes are an entire district of smiths, artificers, rune-carvers, and ancient craft-houses within Valhalla.

This is where:

- Weapons are reforged
- Armor is renewed
- Constructs are studied
- Corewright knowledge intersects with older war technology

## Establishing Shot

Heat rolls through the Forge-Lanes in waves, carrying the ring of hammers, the hiss of quenching steel, and the glow of rune-fire from open workshops. Massive anvils sit beneath carved beams blackened by centuries of sparks. Smiths work with practiced fury while dragon-headed bellows breathe flame into forges that burn hotter than any mortal fire. Behind some sealed doors, the metal does not ring — it hums.

## Function

- Upgrade hub
- Craft district
- Corewright integration point
- Ancient war-tech lore site
- Moral pressure around creation and destruction

## Hidden Truth

Some craftsmen remember making weapons that caused too much destruction.

There are sealed projects no one will talk about.

Corewright characters may feel:

- Recognition
- Unease
- Possibly fear

## GM Note

The Forge should make upgrades feel exciting, but never morally weightless. This is where “can we make it stronger?” starts becoming “should we?”
