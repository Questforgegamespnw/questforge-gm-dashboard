# The Golden Fields

> “Even in eternity… something must still be grown.”

## Overview

The Golden Fields are endless fields of divine wheat and root-fed crops grown from Yggdrasil’s influence.

This is where:

- Sustenance is created
- Balance is maintained
- The health of the system can be seen before warriors understand it

The Fields are not a break from the war machine. They are one of the quiet systems keeping it alive.

## Establishing Shot

The road opens onto fields of wheat so golden they seem to hold their own light. The grain moves in slow, rhythmic waves even when the wind does not match it, and distant farmers work in steady cadence beneath a sky that never fully darkens. At the edge of sight, the roots of Yggdrasil are suggested more than seen — a vast presence beneath the soil, feeding everything.

## Function

- World-state indicator
- Sustenance source
- Subtle corruption foreshadowing
- Quiet RP zone
- Grounded perspective outside the warrior culture

## Hidden Truth

The crops are tied directly to:

- Root health
- Sap purity
- Yggdrasil’s sustaining force

At higher escalation, the wheat begins to:

- Pale
- Crack
- Bleed sap-like resin
- Grow in wrong patterns
- Respond to invisible pressure

## GM Note

Farmers should notice problems before heroes do. The Fields are where cosmic failure becomes agricultural.
