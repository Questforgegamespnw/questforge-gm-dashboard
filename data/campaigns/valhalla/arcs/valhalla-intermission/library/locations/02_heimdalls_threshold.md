# Heimdall’s Threshold

> “He sees. He knows. He does not explain.”

## Overview

Heimdall’s Threshold is the still, silent space at the Bifrost edge.

It is one of the most important non-combat locations in Valhalla because it functions as a system indicator through Heimdall’s behavior.

## Function

- Tone anchor
- System indicator
- Foreshadow delivery point
- Return acknowledgment
- Threshold between mission violence and hub life

## Establishing Shot

At the edge of the Bifrost platform, the color of the return-light thins into stillness. Heimdall stands where he always stands, unmoving, framed by gold and impossible distance. Light reflects strangely from his armor, as if it is arriving from more than one direction at once. His gaze is already on the party before they fully rise.

## Core Rule

If Heimdall says less than usual:  
**Something is wrong.**

If Heimdall says nothing:  
**Something is very wrong.**

## Standard Acknowledgment

> “You return. Good.”

## GM Use

Use Heimdall to signal system health through restraint, not exposition.

He should not over-explain. His silence, brevity, and changed ritual are the warning.

## Escalation Profile

### Stable

Heimdall acknowledges the party with calm certainty.

### Strain

He speaks less than usual. His acknowledgment is shorter, delayed, or unusually focused.

### Fracture

He pauses before speaking. He watches one player longer than the others. He appears to know something he chooses not to say.

### Break

He says nothing. The silence is the message.
