# The Valkyrie Aerie

> “They choose the fallen. They carry the worthy. They do not fail.”

## Overview

The Valkyrie Aerie is a vast network of open-air platforms, sky bridges, and roosting spires suspended above Valhalla.

This is where:

- Valkyries deploy
- Souls are retrieved
- Missions are assigned and tracked
- Winged mounts rest and launch
- The first signs of retrieval failure become visible

Normally, the Aerie is disciplined, efficient, and almost serene.

Now, cracks are beginning to show.

## Establishing Shot

High above the fortress, open-air platforms hang in the golden sky, joined by narrow bridges and rising spires. Valkyries stand between missions in composed silence while winged mounts circle below in slow, controlled arcs. Banners snap in the wind, each marking a sortie path beyond Valhalla. The view should feel impossible and orderly — until the party notices the flight paths are no longer perfectly aligned.

## Hidden Truth

The Valkyries are:

- Making more sorties than ever before
- Traveling further from Valhalla
- Retrieving souls that do not always respond correctly
- Beginning to miss some souls entirely

They are tired.  
They are divided.  
Some are beginning to question who should be chosen.

## Foreshadow: The Dark Valkyrie

A Valkyrie who:

- Has made too many retrievals
- Has seen too many failed returns
- Begins to question the system

### Early Signs

One Valkyrie:

- Lingers after missions
- Watches the party closely
- Asks strange questions

Example:

> “When you fell… did it hurt longer than it should have?”

### Mid-Tier Behavior

- Stops retrieving certain souls
- Leaves some behind intentionally
- Begins choosing who is “worth saving”

### Late-Tier Transformation

Becomes:

> A Dark Valkyrie — Arbiter of the Broken Cycle

She no longer retrieves souls.  
She intercepts them.  
She redirects them elsewhere.

## System Chain

```text
Root Corruption →
Souls destabilize →
Valkyries overworked →
Retrieval failures →
Quiet Rows break →
Valhalla destabilizes
```

The Aerie is the middle link in the chain.

## Final Design Truth

The Valkyries are the system trying to hold together a world that is coming apart.

And when they break, everything breaks with them.
