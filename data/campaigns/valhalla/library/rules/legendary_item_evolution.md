# Legendary Item Evolution

> “Your power does not grow with you. It grows with the end of the world.”

## Core Rule

Legendary items in this campaign do not scale with character level.

They evolve based on the Ragnarok Escalation Tier.

## Design Purpose

This system ensures:

- Power feels tied to the story, not XP
- Players feel the world changing through mechanics
- Progression is shared and thematic
- Escalation feels dangerous and empowering

## Trigger

When the Ragnarok Meter enters a new tier:

- Tier 2: 4–6
- Tier 3: 7–9
- Tier 4: 10

All legendary items awaken new properties.

## Important Rule

This is:

- Automatic
- Narrative-driven
- Not optional

## In-Lore Explanation

Items are not leveling up.

They are responding to instability in Yggdrasil.

Optional NPC line from Sven or Brokk:

> “That’s not your weapon changing…”
>
> “That’s the world getting worse.”

## Tier Breakdown

| Tier | State | Item Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 / 1–3 | Dormant Power | Items function at base legendary level; strong but controlled |
| Tier 2 / 4–6 | Awakening | New abilities unlock, existing effects improve, items react to events |
| Tier 3 / 7–9 | Surging Power | Major upgrades activate, battlefield-altering effects appear, items feel unstable |
| Tier 4 / 10 | Mythic State | Peak power unlocked, reality-bending effects, possible drawbacks |

## Design Principles

### Do

- Reinforce identity
- Add options, not just damage
- Interact with battlefield and world state

### Do Not

- Tie abilities to level gates
- Make upgrades feel arbitrary
- Outscale the narrative

## Player Experience

At first:

> “We are strong.”

Then:

> “We are getting stronger…”

Then:

> “Why are we getting stronger?”

Finally:

> “This is not a good sign.”

## Optional Instability Side Effects at Tier 3+

Examples:

- Weapons shift or glow incorrectly
- Minor unpredictable effects
- Emotional or sensory feedback
- Bard’s voice echoes slightly out of sync
- Rogue flickers briefly during movement
- Corewright constructs hesitate, then overperform

## System Integration

Connects to:

- Ragnarok Meter
- Root corruption
- Valhalla instability
- Mortality Drift

## Final Truth

The players are not just getting stronger.

They are adapting to a world that is breaking.

## Final DM Tool

When a player gets excited about a new upgrade, smile and remember:

> “This is happening because things are getting worse.”
