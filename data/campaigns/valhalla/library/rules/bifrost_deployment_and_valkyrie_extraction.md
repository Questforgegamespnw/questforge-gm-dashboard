# Bifrost Deployment & Valkyrie Extraction

> “You fall into war. You are taken from it.”

## Core Concept

The party does not travel traditionally.

They are:

- Deployed via the Bifrost
- Extracted by Valkyries

## Design Purpose

This system creates:

- Fast pacing
- Cinematic entry and exit every mission
- Controlled mission structure
- Built-in tension
- Extraction that is not guaranteed

## Bifrost Down — Deployment

Players are:

- Locked onto a target location
- Pulled through the Bifrost
- Dropped directly into the mission zone

## Standard Deployment Text

> The sky tears open in radiant color—
>
> Light becomes motion.
> Motion becomes force.
>
> You are no longer falling—
> you are being delivered.
>
> Impact comes fast.
>
> Snow—stone—earth—
>
> The world slams back into existence around you.
>
> And the mission has already begun.

## Deployment Mechanics

- Instant insertion into active scenario
- No stealth entry unless explicitly designed
- Often lands them mid-conflict

## DM Rule

The world does not wait for them.

They arrive into:

- Ongoing battles
- Active threats
- Already failing situations

## Deployment Escalation

| Tier | Effect |
|---|---|
| Tier 1–2 / Stable | Clean drop, accurate positioning, minimal distortion |
| Tier 3 / Unstable | Rough landing, slight scatter, sensory distortion |
| Tier 4 / Breaking | Misdrops, wrong location/altitude/timing, split party risk, hostile arrival |

## Valkyrie Up — Extraction

Extraction is not automatic.

A Valkyrie must:

- Reach the party
- Secure them
- Pull them out

## Standard Extraction Text

> The battle fades—
>
> Not because it ends…
>
> But because something cuts through it.
>
> A streak of light—
> Wings—steel—impact—
>
> A Valkyrie lands among you.
>
> “Move.”

## Base Extraction Rule

Extraction requires either:

- Mission state stabilized
- Emergency retrieval triggered

## Extraction Types

### Clean Extraction

- Mission complete
- Valkyrie arrives calmly
- Full team extracted

### Hot Extraction

- Combat still active
- Valkyrie lands mid-fight
- Players must reach an extraction zone and survive until lift

### Forced Extraction

Triggered by:

- Near TPK
- Critical objective failure
- Major escalation spike

Effect:

- Violent pull-out
- Incomplete control
- Possible consequences

### Failed Extraction

Rare but powerful.

- Valkyrie delayed or blocked
- Players must survive longer
- Players may need to solve the situation themselves

Do not overuse failure. When it happens, it should hit hard.

## Optional Extraction Mechanics

Define an extraction zone:

- Gate
- Clearing
- Shrine
- Battlefield anchor

Players must reach it and hold it.

Suggested hold timer:

- 2–3 rounds

During extraction:

- Enemies surge
- Chaos spikes
- Stakes peak

## Heimdall Interaction

Heimdall tracks:

- Who deploys
- Who returns
- Who returns wrong

### Return Lines

| State | Line |
|---|---|
| Normal | “You return.” |
| Minor anomaly | “You return… mostly.” |
| Major warning | “You return. But something followed.” |
| Critical failure | Silence |

Silence is the panic button.

## Valkyrie Extraction Tone

Different Valkyries change the extraction feel.

| Valkyrie | Tone | Line |
|---|---|---|
| Ryndis | Efficient, blunt | “You’re alive. Good enough.” |
| Thora | Aggressive | “Move or get left!” |
| Kara | Quiet, unsettling | “…You’re different.” |
| Eldri | Gentle, concerned | “Stay with me… don’t slip.” |
| Sylva | Encouraging | “You made it! I knew you would!” |

## Ragnarok Integration

Extraction reliability degrades over time.

| Tier | Reliability |
|---|---|
| Tier 1–2 | Reliable, safe, expected |
| Tier 3 | Delays, strain, stressed Valkyries |
| Tier 4 | Failures, partial extractions, something can follow back |

## Critical Design Truth

This system reinforces:

- “We are deployed weapons.”
- “We are not always safe.”
- “Return is not guaranteed.”

## Final DM Tool

Whenever tension dips, ask:

> “What would make extraction harder right now?”

Then apply:

- Time pressure
- Enemy surge
- Valkyrie delay
- Environmental collapse
