# Multi-Pantheon Character Support

> “You were not meant to be here. That is why you are valuable.”

## Core Rule

Players are not limited to Norse gods, Norse beliefs, or Norse spiritual origins.

They may choose:

- Any deity
- Any pantheon
- Any spiritual origin
- Any philosophical origin
- No religious devotion at all

## Player-Facing Explanation

When players ask:

> “Why am I in Valhalla if I do not follow Norse gods?”

A Valkyrie can answer:

> “You are… on loan.”
>
> “War recognizes war. Victory recognizes victory.”
>
> “Your soul was claimed by a domain aligned with ours.”

## Simple Truth

Valhalla is not claiming ownership.

It is borrowing champions from aligned domains.

## DM Layer

Different afterlives exist simultaneously.

Each governs:

- Its own dead
- Its own rules
- Its own philosophy

Valkyries can temporarily requisition souls, deploy them as Einherjar, and return them when service is complete.

## Why These Players?

They meet the criteria:

- Combat capability
- Strong will / identity
- A worthy death aligned with conflict, sacrifice, protection, or victory

## Player Freedom Benefits

This allows players to be:

- A servant of a sun god
- A war cleric of another pantheon
- A rogue blessed by trickster spirits
- A completely non-religious hero
- Someone whose original afterlife was not Valhalla

## Roleplay Tension

Useful player questions:

- “This is not my afterlife.”
- “Why was I chosen?”
- “Do I go back?”
- “Who gave Valhalla permission?”

## Player Outlook Branching

### Acceptance

> “I serve here now.”

Effect: Easier integration with Valkyries.

### Conflicted

> “This is not where I belong.”

Effect: Strong RP tension and good Hel/Loki hooks.

### Resistant

> “I want to go back.”

Effect: Future arc driver and possible faction conflict.

## Optional Player Prompt

Ask each player:

> “What would your original afterlife have been like?”

This allows later contrast between Valhalla and their true destination.

## Long-Term Payoff

This system allows:

- Diverse characters without lore breaks
- Philosophical conflict between gods
- Personal stakes beyond saving the world
- Recall attempts from other divine agents
- Doubt about whether Valhalla has the right to use them

## Hooks

### Heimdall

> “You are not the only ones who walk between.”

### Loki

> “You don’t even belong to this story. That’s what makes you interesting.”

### Hel

> “You were already meant to rest… somewhere else.”

## Final Truth

Valhalla is not their home.

It is their assignment.

## Final DM Line

When a player questions the arrangement:

> “When this is over, you will go where you were always meant to.”
>
> “If that place still exists.”
