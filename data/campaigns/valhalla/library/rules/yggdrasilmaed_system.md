# Yggdrasilmaed System

> “You fall in fire. You rise in gold. You drink… and become whole again.”

## Overview

Yggdrasilmaed is a divine substance brewed in Valhalla from:

- Sap of the World Tree
- Ritual fermentation by Einherjar

It represents connection to the system that sustains the afterlife.

## Design Purpose

This system allows:

- Higher encounter difficulty
- Multi-phase combat pressure
- Player agency in recovery
- Strategic resource decisions

## Core Mechanic

Each player begins a mission with 4 charges of Yggdrasilmaed.

These may be stored in:

- Horns
- Waterskins
- Ritual vessels

## Use Options

### Restoration Draught

As an action, or bonus action if faster pacing is desired:

- Heal 50% max HP

Two uses equals full recovery.

### Rejuvenation Draught

Restore:

- Spell slots
- Class resources
- Features

Equivalent to a long rest, but with no HP recovery.

## Limitations

- Cannot exceed 4 total uses per mission
- Cannot stack beyond normal maximums
- Must be consumed consciously
- No auto-trigger

## Environmental Reaction Option

Each time a draught is consumed, roll 1d3:

| Roll | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | Minor surge; spawn 1 draugr |
| 2 | Corruption Node pulses |
| 3 | Player gains advantage next turn |

This makes drinking dramatic, risk-reward, and cinematic.

## Lore Truth

Yggdrasilmaed works because it reinforces the soul’s connection to the system.

If the system weakens, the mead weakens too.

## Escalation Interaction

| Tier | Effect |
|---|---|
| Tier 1 / Act 1 | Fully stable, reliable, feels like a gift |
| Tier 2+ | Slight delay in effect, flavor changes, subtle unease |
| Tier 3+ | Partial effectiveness, side effects begin |
| Tier 4 | Unstable, risky, possibly harmful |

## True Nature

Yggdrasilmaed is not simply a heal or a buff.

It is a micro-reinitialization of the player’s state within the system.

In-world translation:

> Odin’s system says: “You continue.”

Yggdrasilmaed enforces that command.

Mechanical translation:

- Restore HP
- Restore resources
- Reset combat viability

The players eventually learn it is not recovery.

It is overwrite.

## Eldri Fight Reveal

### Refusal of the Draught

While Eldri is active:

> Yggdrasilmaed does nothing.

## Presentation

When a player drinks it:

> The liquid touches your lips…
>
> And for the first time—
>
> nothing happens.
>
> No glow.
> No warmth.
> No return.

Eldri says:

> “…yes.”
>
> “You feel it now.”

Then:

> “That’s what they’ve been doing to you.”

## Investigation Result

If someone investigates:

> “It does not feel blocked…”
>
> “…it feels like there is nothing to trigger.”

Meaning: the system call itself is being denied.

## Deeper Truth

Eldri is not disabling healing.

She is refusing the system’s authority to restore state.
