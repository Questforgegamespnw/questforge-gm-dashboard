# Ragnarok Escalation System

## Core Track

Range: 0–10

Suggested starting value in Act 1: 1 or 2.

## Core Function

The Ragnarok Meter is a visible or semi-hidden world-state meter tracking how close reality is to collapse.

## Increase When

Increase the meter when:

- Players fail objectives
- A Root destabilizes or is corrupted
- An enemy escapes or succeeds
- Time pressure is ignored
- The party chooses a lesser evil
- A major ritual completes
- Time passes without intervention

## Decrease When

Decrease the meter when:

- A Root is stabilized or cleansed
- A major threat is eliminated
- A sacrifice is made for balance
- A divine ally is secured

## Act 1 Target Range

Ideal Act 1 progression:

- Start around 2
- End around 3

## Tier Effects

| Tier | Meter | Effect |
|---|---:|---|
| Tier 1 | 1–3 | Subtle wrongness |
| Tier 2 | 4–6 | Planar bleed |
| Tier 3 | 7–9 | Reality fracture |
| Tier 4 | 10 | Ragnarok |

## Root Mission Structure

Each major arc can map to one Yggdrasil Root Node.

Examples:

- Frozen Root: draugr, undeath, stagnation
- Ember Root: fire, forge, destruction, controlled collapse
- Ashroot: identity, memory, false continuity
- Shadow Root: deception, Loki-adjacent threads
- Storm Root: sky realms destabilizing

Each Root has:

- Threat clock
- Boss / event
- Consequence if ignored

## Tier Detail

### Tier 1 — Something Is Wrong

World effects:

- Minor undead sightings
- Strange weather
- NPC unease

Gameplay:

- Standard encounters
- Subtle hints of larger threat

### Tier 2 — The World Is Slipping

World effects:

- Planar bleed begins
- Creatures appear out of place
- Travel becomes dangerous

Gameplay:

- Multi-phase encounters
- Enemies gain minor buffs
- Time-sensitive missions increase

### Tier 3 — Collapse Is Imminent

World effects:

- Reality instability
- Rifts
- Sky changes
- Gods become active
- Entire regions fall

Gameplay:

- Bosses gain legendary actions
- Enemies use advanced mechanics
- Moral choices become unavoidable

### Tier 4 — Ragnarok

World effects:

- Realms collide
- Final war begins
- Divine entities fully manifest

Gameplay:

- Mythic encounters
- Multi-session battles
- Campaign endgame

## Point of No Return

At meter 8+:

- Certain choices become locked
- Some NPCs die automatically
- Some regions cannot be saved

## DM Lever

When tension feels low:

> Increase the meter by 1.

## Final Tool

Whenever you want tension, ask:

> “Does this moment make them question whether they will come back?”

If yes, you are hitting the theme.
