# Bifrost Platform

> “Arrival is always violent. It is no longer always clean.”

## Overview

The Bifrost Platform is the players’ standard return point after missions. It is the place where battlefield chaos gives way to Valhalla’s golden order.

The return should feel familiar, powerful, and ritualized — but not always clean.

## Function

- Session opener
- Post-mission return
- Recap point
- Tone reset
- Emotional decompression
- First anomaly indicator

## Establishing Shot

The players fall in streaks of burning rainbow light, striking the platform with a force that should shatter stone but never does. Heat flashes through them and then vanishes instantly, replaced by warm air, distant laughter, woodsmoke, bread, and mead. The impact is familiar. Controlled. Still violent. And each return feels just a little different, as if Valhalla is adjusting itself around them.

## Presentation

### Vibe

Violent arrival contained by divine order.

### Sensory Details

- Burning rainbow light
- Heat fading instantly into warmth
- Sound of battle replaced by distant laughter
- Smell of blood replaced by bread, mead, and woodsmoke
- Landing stones humming faintly after impact

## Subtle Detail

The landing site always feels slightly different.

Not wrong at first. Just adjusted.

Like Valhalla is correcting for something the players cannot see.

## GM Use

Use the Bifrost Platform as:

- Recap moment
- Tone reset
- Emotional decompression
- System foreshadowing
- First check of whether the return cycle is still clean

## Escalation Profile

### Stable

Arrival is violent but controlled. The platform receives the party cleanly.

### Strain

Impact feels harsher. Warmth takes a moment longer to arrive. The platform hums after the return.

### Fracture

One character lands slightly out of sync. Sound from the mission lingers too long. The smell of blood does not fade immediately.

### Break

The return is incomplete, delayed, or visibly misaligned. Valhalla receives the party, but not cleanly.
