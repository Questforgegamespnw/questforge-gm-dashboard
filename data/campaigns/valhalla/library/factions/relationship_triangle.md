# Relationship Triangle

## Core Divine/Cosmic Axes

### Yggdrasil System

- Order
- Continuation

### Valkyries

- Duty
- Fracture

### Hel

- Rest
- Acceptance

### Loki

- Change
- Choice

## Player Role

The players decide which direction the world bends.

## Use at Table

Use this as a zoomed-out compass for divine and philosophical pressure.

The triangle is not meant to overexplain metaphysics.

It gives you a quick way to decide what each major force wants from a scene:

- Yggdrasil wants continuity.
- Valkyries want duty to keep working.
- Hel wants rest and acceptance.
- Loki wants choice and change.
- The players decide which claim matters most.
