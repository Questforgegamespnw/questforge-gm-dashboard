## Common Player Questions / Answer Bank

Use these when the players want practical answers about death, retrieval, survival, and what the Valkyries actually experience in the field.

### Who are you?

“Ryndis. I retrieve the fallen.”

She glances over their wounds before their faces.

“Usually.”

**GM delivery note:** That final word is the pressure point.

### What do you actually do?

“I go where the dying end up, find the ones still reachable, and bring them back before the world finishes taking them apart.”

If asked whether that is hard:

“It is not supposed to be easy. It is supposed to be possible.”

**GM delivery note:** Keep it dry. The horror is in the practical details.

### What does retrieval feel like?

“Like reaching into a storm for a hand that may not know it has fingers anymore.”

She shrugs.

“On a good day, they hear you. On a bad day, you carry what answers.”

**GM delivery note:** This is a strong answer for making retrieval human and frightening.

### Are deaths safe here?

“No.”

A beat.

“They are safer than elsewhere. That is not the same thing.”

**GM delivery note:** Let this puncture player assumptions without making her dramatic.

### Which deaths are hardest to retrieve?

“Isolated deaths. Forgotten deaths. Deaths in places that do not want witnesses.”

If pressed:

“And lately? Deaths that should have been simple.”

**GM delivery note:** Foreshadows narrowing retrieval windows and unstable zones.

### Why tell us not to die where you can’t reach us?

“Because bravery does not shorten distance.”

She points toward the mission platforms.

“You die somewhere clean, I can work. You die somewhere wrong, I start guessing. I dislike guessing.”

**GM delivery note:** Practical, almost funny, but serious.

### What is going wrong?

“Routes are changing. Some zones resist retrieval. Some souls arrive thin.”

She looks toward the Aerie edge.

“I am marking patterns. Command can call them exceptions for now.”

**GM delivery note:** Ryndis should sound like she has data before anyone wants to admit the conclusion.

### Do you trust command?

“I trust Svinhild to make the hard call.”

A pause.

“I do not trust any system that asks for fewer questions when the bodies get stranger.”

**GM delivery note:** Early quiet dissenter energy.

### Do you trust us?

“You are still alive enough to ask questions. That puts you ahead of most.”

If the party has made smart survival choices:

“You learn. That is rarer than courage.”

**GM delivery note:** Ryndis approval should feel earned and understated.

### What should we do next?

“Learn your exits. Mark your dead. Tell the truth when something feels wrong.”

If mission choice is pending:

“Pick the job where delay kills the most people.”

**GM delivery note:** Good for converting roleplay into actionable table movement.

### What do you think of the other Valkyries?

- **Svinhild:** “Carries command like armor. Heavy armor.”
- **Eldri:** “Stronger than the loud ones.”
- **Thora:** “Fast enough to survive mistakes. For now.”
- **Kara:** “Sees things. Says half of them. Dangerous habit.”
- **Sylva:** “Good heart. Needs one bad day.”
