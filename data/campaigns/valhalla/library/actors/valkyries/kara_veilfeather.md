# Kara Veilfeather — The Watcher

> “You changed.”

## Overview

A quiet Valkyrie who observes everything and speaks only when it matters.

**Role:** Watcher / cryptic truth-dropper / independent Valkyrie  
**Group:** Valkyries  
**Likely Location:** `location_valhalla_valkyrie_aerie`  
**Current State:** Quiet, unsettlingly aware, and likely working independently.

## Presence

- **Vibe:** Cryptic perception with dangerous independence.
- **Physicality:** Still at the edge of scenes; watches before speaking; appears to have already noticed the change everyone else missed.
- **Voice:** Quiet, sparse, delayed; sometimes only a pause and a sentence.
- **Scene Impact:** Seeds doubt with very few words.
- **Visual Anchor:** A watcher’s stillness and a blade that catches cold light.

## Personality / Use at Table

Use Kara Veilfeather when you need:

- Watcher / cryptic truth-dropper / independent Valkyrie
- A scene shaped by: Cryptic perception with dangerous independence.
- A delivery cue grounded in: Seeds doubt with very few words.

## Motivation

- **Wants:** Understand what is changing and act before command admits it.
- **Fears:** That official order will preserve the system long enough for it to fail catastrophically.
- **Leverage:** Already knows more than most and is willing to move independently.

## Knowledge

### Secrets

## Common Player Questions / Answer Bank

Use Kara sparingly. Her answers should create tension and better questions, not solve the mystery outright.

### Who are you?

A pause.

“Kara.”

Another pause.

“I watch.”

**GM delivery note:** Do not fill the silence. Let the players decide whether to ask more.

### What do you actually do?

“I notice what still moves after everyone says the moment has passed.”

If they ask if that is a job:

“Sometimes.”

**GM delivery note:** Kara is not being cute. She is avoiding institutional language.

### Why are you staring at us?

“You changed.”

If they ask how:

“Not evenly.”

**GM delivery note:** This should make the party look at each other.

### What do you mean, not all of us returned?

“I did not say all.”

If they call that evasive:

“No. I was precise.”

**GM delivery note:** Kara should be infuriatingly careful.

### What did you see?

“Less than Heimdall.”

A pause.

“More than I was told to see.”

**GM delivery note:** Tie her to Heimdall without making her an exposition source.

### What is going wrong?

“The shape is still standing. The inside is not.”

If pressed:

“Valhalla is very good at looking whole.”

**GM delivery note:** This is a clean thesis line for system failure.

### Do you trust command?

“No.”

A pause.

“I trust some commanders.”

**GM delivery note:** Kara separates people from systems.

### Do you trust us?

“I am watching.”

If the party has been honest:

“That is not a no.”

If the party has hidden things:

“That is not a yes.”

**GM delivery note:** She grants almost nothing directly.

### What are you afraid of?

“Orders that arrive after truth.”

If the players ask what that means:

“It means obedience can be late.”

**GM delivery note:** Dark Valkyrie foreshadow. She may act independently because command is too slow.

### What should we do next?

“Ask who benefits from everyone staying calm.”

If mission choice is pending:

“Choose the place that command describes most cleanly. That is where something has been edited out.”

**GM delivery note:** Kara pushes suspicion without making the choice for them.

### Are you becoming something else?

Silence.

“Everyone is.”

**GM delivery note:** This can point toward Dark Valkyrie tension without confirming it.

### What do you think of the other Valkyries?

- **Svinhild:** “Carries the map. Not the territory.”
- **Ryndis:** “Counts what others call exceptions.”
- **Eldri:** “Still hears what is almost gone.”
- **Thora:** “Runs from the moment after.”
- **Sylva:** “Believes loudly. That makes silence crueler.”
