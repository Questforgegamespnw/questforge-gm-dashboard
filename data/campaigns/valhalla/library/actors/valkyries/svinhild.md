## Common Player Questions / Answer Bank

Use these when the players slow the Aerie scene down and start treating Svinhild like a person instead of a quest marker.

### Who are you?

**Short answer:** “Svinhild. Valkyrie commander. If you need a longer title, you have too much free time.”

**Full answer:**  
“I coordinate sorties, assign retrieval support, and make sure warriors arrive where they can still matter. I am not here to inspire you. I am here to make sure your courage lands somewhere useful.”

**GM delivery note:** Clipped, clean, no warmth unless the players have earned it. She is not rude for sport; she is rationing attention.

### What do you actually do?

“I decide where force is needed before everyone agrees it is needed. Then I send people into danger and accept responsibility for the cost.”

If the players challenge that:

“Do not mistake command for distance. Every name I assign may become a name someone else has to carve.”

**GM delivery note:** Let her sound hard, then reveal that the hardness is load-bearing.

### Why keep pushing?

“Because stopping does not make the need smaller.”

She looks toward the platforms where the winged mounts circle.

“It only means someone else reaches the dead too late.”

**GM delivery note:** This is the core Svinhild answer. Harsh but not cruel.

### Are we winning?

She does not answer immediately.

“We are still moving.”

If pressed:

“That is not the same thing. I know.”

**GM delivery note:** This is the crack in the armor. Use it when the players ask directly and deserve a truthful answer.

### Do you trust us?

“I trust what you have survived. I trust results. I do not yet know whether I trust your judgment.”

If the party has completed a costly mission:

“You return damaged and still useful. That is not nothing.”

**GM delivery note:** Svinhild does not hand out emotional approval, but practical respect should feel meaningful.

### What are you afraid of?

“That discipline will not be enough.”

She says it flatly, like a report.

“That we will make every correct decision and still arrive too late.”

**GM delivery note:** This should feel like a confession she did not mean to make.

### What is going wrong?

“Sorties are stretching longer. Retrieval windows are narrowing. Briefings that should be routine are becoming exceptions.”

She fixes them with a level look.

“That is what you need to know today.”

**GM delivery note:** She reveals operational symptoms, not cosmic truth.

### What do you want from us?

“Be useful. Be honest in your reports. Do not make me guess what killed you.”

If softened:

“And if you see something wrong before I do, speak.”

**GM delivery note:** Gives players a clear operating role in the system.

### What do you think we should do next?

“Choose the mission that stops the most bleeding.”

If multiple missions are available:

“If you cannot decide by certainty, decide by consequence.”

**GM delivery note:** Good transition into mission selection.

### What do you think of the other Valkyries?

- **Ryndis:** “Useful because she does not dramatize fear.”
- **Eldri:** “Necessary. More necessary than most warriors understand.”
- **Thora:** “Fast. Effective. Becoming expensive.”
- **Kara:** “She watches too much and reports too little.”
- **Sylva:** “Hope is useful. Untested hope is fragile.”

**GM delivery note:** These are short relationship reads, not gossip.
