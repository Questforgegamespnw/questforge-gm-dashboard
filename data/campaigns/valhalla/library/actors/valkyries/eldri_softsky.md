# Eldri Softsky — The Gentle Guide

> “It’s alright… you made it back.”

## Overview

A guide for confused or fragmented souls.

**Role:** Gentle guide / emotional anchor / fragmented soul specialist  
**Group:** Valkyries  
**Likely Location:** `location_valhalla_valkyrie_aerie`  
**Current State:** Gentle and patient, but frightened by souls that no longer respond to her voice.

## Presence

- **Vibe:** Deep empathy under quiet terror.
- **Physicality:** Approaches slowly, never crowds, hands visible, movements softened as if guiding someone from a nightmare.
- **Voice:** Soft-spoken, patient, intimate; built for reassurance.
- **Scene Impact:** Makes failed return feel personal and humane.
- **Visual Anchor:** A gentle presence that feels like the first safe voice after death.

## Common Player Questions / Answer Bank

Use these when players ask about trauma, fragmented souls, return confusion, comfort, memory, or what it means when a soul does not come back clean.

### Who are you?

“Eldri.”

She gives the name softly, like offering a hand.

“I help the ones who return frightened, confused, or not quite gathered into themselves.”

**GM delivery note:** She should feel safe before she feels afraid.

### What do you actually do?

“I speak to the newly returned until they remember that they are more than the moment that killed them.”

She does not rush the next sentence.

“Sometimes that means a name. Sometimes a song. Sometimes just staying near enough that they are not alone when memory comes back.”

**GM delivery note:** Make her work concrete and intimate.

### What happens to confused souls?

“Most are only lost for a little while.”

She smiles, but it is tired.

“They hear a voice. They remember a hand. They find their name again.”

If pressed:

“Most.”

**GM delivery note:** The single word “Most” is the fracture.

### Why does comfort matter?

“Because terror teaches the soul to curl inward.”

She folds her hands.

“If the first voice after death is gentle, sometimes the soul believes it is safe enough to become whole again.”

**GM delivery note:** This is Eldri’s philosophy and why she matters.

### What does it look like when someone does not return clean?

“They answer to the wrong name. Or no name.”

She looks down.

“They remember pain before they remember love. Sometimes they wake as if the battlefield is still happening.”

**GM delivery note:** Keep it humane, not clinical.

### What is going wrong?

“Some do not hear me anymore.”

She says it very quietly.

“I can be beside them. I can say their name. I can hold their hand. And something in them is too far away to answer.”

**GM delivery note:** This is her fear. Let it land.

### Are we in danger?

“Yes.”

Then, gentler:

“But danger is not the same thing as abandonment.”

**GM delivery note:** Eldri reassures without lying.

### Do you trust us?

“I trust that you came back carrying more than wounds.”

If the players have shown care for others:

“And I trust people who notice when someone else is afraid.”

**GM delivery note:** Eldri trusts compassion and attention more than success.

### What are you afraid of?

“That one day I will say a name and feel nothing answer.”

A small pause.

“And that I will keep saying it anyway.”

**GM delivery note:** This is a tragic-anchor line.

### What should we do next?

“Listen for what people do not say after they return.”

If tying to Quiet Rows:

“And if a name feels wrong, do not ignore it.”

**GM delivery note:** Good bridge to Quiet Rows or return instability.

### What do you think of the other Valkyries?

- **Svinhild:** “She carries fear by turning it into orders.”
- **Ryndis:** “She sounds hard because she has brought back too many broken things.”
- **Thora:** “She runs before grief can catch her.”
- **Kara:** “She hears silence too clearly.”
- **Sylva:** “I hope she keeps believing. I worry what it will cost her.”

## Escalation Responses
