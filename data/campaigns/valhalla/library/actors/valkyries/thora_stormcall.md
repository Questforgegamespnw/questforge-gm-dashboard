## Common Player Questions / Answer Bank

Use these when players challenge Thora’s recklessness, ask about speed, provoke her confidence, or try to see what fear is hiding under the bravado.

### Who are you?

“Thora Stormcall.”

She grins like a dare.

“If something goes wrong fast, I get there faster.”

**GM delivery note:** Confident, teasing, forward momentum.

### What do you actually do?

“I reach the places everyone else is still discussing.”

She taps the butt of her spear once.

“Sometimes that means retrieval. Sometimes that means making the thing blocking retrieval regret standing there.”

**GM delivery note:** Thora frames action as rescue, not just violence.

### Why are you always in such a hurry?

“Because souls do not wait politely while command finishes a sentence.”

If challenged:

“Delay kills. I prefer killing the delay.”

**GM delivery note:** This is the clean version of her philosophy.

### Are you reckless?

“Only if I am wrong.”

She smiles.

“I am usually not wrong.”

If the players push harder:

“Fine. Sometimes I am early.”

**GM delivery note:** Let her dodge the real fear with style.

### What are you afraid of?

“Slow doors. Bad orders. People who call hesitation wisdom.”

If pressed in a quieter moment:

“That I will arrive one breath late.”

**GM delivery note:** That last line is the truth under the swagger.

### What is going wrong?

“Zones are getting uglier. Edges move. Things that should stay dead get opinions.”

She spins her spear once.

“So we move faster.”

**GM delivery note:** She interprets system failure as a speed problem.

### Do you trust command?

“I trust Svinhild to point me at something worth hitting.”

A beat.

“I trust myself to know when pointing is taking too long.”

**GM delivery note:** This is where command tension shows.

### Do you trust us?

“Depends. Do you slow down when scared?”

If the party has acted boldly:

“You might be fun.”

If they have been cautious:

“You might live. Different virtue.”

**GM delivery note:** Thora respects courage and competence, but can learn to respect caution.

### What should we do next?

“Pick the mission everyone is hoping someone else handles.”

She leans forward.

“That is usually where the truth is bleeding.”

**GM delivery note:** Good transition to dangerous mission selection.

### Why risk yourself?

“Because someone has to be first.”

If softened:

“And because second sometimes means carrying what first could have saved.”

**GM delivery note:** Action hero line with tragedy underneath.

### What do you think of the other Valkyries?

- **Svinhild:** “Best commander to disobey intelligently.”
- **Ryndis:** “Acts bored so nobody notices she is worried.”
- **Eldri:** “Braver than she looks. Maybe braver than me.”
- **Kara:** “Creepy. Usually right. Annoying combination.”
- **Sylva:** “Bright little banner. Hope she never sees what banners look like after battle.”
