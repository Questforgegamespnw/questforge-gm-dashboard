# No Bell for Harthrun — Adventure Overview

## Premise

Harthrun reported unfamiliar soldiers approaching under allied appearance. Fort Dios, already engaged on several confirmed fronts, could not verify the report without weakening its defenses and declined immediate aid. The village has now gone silent.

The party is sent to determine what happened, recover survivors, and identify whether a new front has opened. Harthrun has already been broken. The survivors were sorted and taken to the village mine, where an Ichor Legion commander is converting them into silent thralls and testing a command-geas carried through linked military ledgers.

## Run Target

- System: Dungeons & Dragons 5e
- Level: 8–10
- Runtime: about 3 hours
- Shape: investigation → infiltration → rescue crisis → multi-objective boss encounter

## Three-Hour Spine

1. **Fort Dios Briefing — 10 minutes.** Establish the request, triage decision, and mandate.
2. **Approach — 5 minutes.** Show silence, false uniform, and contradictory tracks.
3. **The Village That Bled — 30–40 minutes.** Establish surprise assault, missing population, sorting, and the mineward trail.
4. **Following the Taken — 5 minutes.** Choose speed versus concealment.
5. **Mine Entry — 20–25 minutes.** Bypass or break silent sentries.
6. **Lower Mine — 25–30 minutes.** Free captives, witness conversion, and make the critical rescue choice.
7. **Conversion Chamber — 45–60 minutes.** Resolve captain, demon, captives, spikes, and ledger.
8. **Aftermath — 5 minutes.** Count survivors, contain the network, and make the report.

## GM Truths to Preserve

- Fort Dios made a rational decision under incomplete information. The result is still tragic.
- The attackers first appeared to be allied soldiers. Panic began only after they attacked from inside the village.
- Once resistance ended, the Legion sorted survivors rather than continuing indiscriminate slaughter.
- Conversion is physical impalement by demonic spikes, not an alchemical machine.
- Converted thralls do not speak.
- The subsumed captain speaks because the demon controls him through command-geas rather than standard conversion.
- The operation aims beyond Harthrun: control of the Darksen and allied communications.

## Tracker Discipline

Advance **Conversion Progress** only for consequential delay, rest, or prolonged indecision after the party understands lives are at stake. Raise **Mine Alert** for actual detection, obvious assault, escaped warning, or major disturbance. Raise **Ledger Influence** only when someone meaningfully reads, answers, copies, or transmits through the compromised system.

## Tone

Make the first horror institutional and physical: an understandable choice, an unanswered village, the evidence of organized betrayal. Reveal the overtly demonic process only as the party approaches and enters the mine.
