# Harthrun Incident Truth

## Before the Party Arrives

1. A Legion force approaches Harthrun using allied uniforms, organization, and recognizable patrol behavior.
2. The town constabulary sends a warning to Fort Dios. The report is uncertain: soldiers are present where no unit is expected, but their allegiance and intent are unconfirmed.
3. Fort Dios is under pressure on multiple active fronts. Sending troops would weaken confirmed defensive needs. Command replies that it cannot dispatch aid without verification.
4. The false soldiers enter close enough to control roads, alarm points, and the square.
5. They attack suddenly. Civilians initially flee in all directions because the threat is already inside the village.
6. The constabulary and residents improvise resistance from within Harthrun. It forms late and is overwhelmed by superior organization and force.
7. Once meaningful resistance collapses, the Legion changes behavior. It stops broad killing, separates survivors, strips belongings, restrains groups, and identifies useful bodies.
8. The bell keeper is killed and the warning rope cut before a full regional alarm can be sustained.
9. Captives are marched to the mine. The injured who cannot continue are killed, abandoned, or used for early conversion testing.
10. In the mine, captives are counted and processed toward ichor-spike conversion.
11. A captured field patrol commander from another village is retained as an articulate command node under ledger-geas control.
12. The Ichor Demon begins testing allied command forms and linked ledgers as a route into the Darksen communication network.

## Fort Dios Responsibility

Do not turn Fort Dios into a hidden villain. Its decision was military triage under genuine pressure and incomplete information. The painful question is not “Who secretly wanted Harthrun dead?” It is “What does responsibility mean when a defensible choice leaves a village unaided?”

The reply found with the constabulary should be restrained and professional. It acknowledges the warning, explains that no unit can be released without verification, and requests further confirmation. It becomes heartbreaking because the constabulary kept it through the final defense.

## What the Legion Learned

Harthrun proves that trusted appearance can defeat an alarm faster than overwhelming monstrous force. The mine operation adds the next layer: captured bodies become troops, captured officers become command nodes, and captured communications become roads into allied structure.
