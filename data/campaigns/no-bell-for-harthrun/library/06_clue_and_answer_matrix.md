# Clue and Answer Matrix

Essential conclusions should never depend on one roll or one fireable moment. Give baseline evidence for competent investigation; use checks to improve speed, specificity, or risk.

| Conclusion | Evidence | Required? |
|---|---|---|
| The attackers appeared allied | copied uniform, ordered entry tracks, familiar field behavior | At least one |
| The attack became a surprise massacre | civilian flight crossing formation tracks, late inward-facing barricades | At least one |
| Fort Dios received the warning | briefing request, reply beside the constabulary dead | Request guaranteed; reply missable |
| Many villagers remain unaccounted for | occupied homes, population records, too few bodies | Guaranteed |
| Survivors were sorted and taken | separated belongings, bindings, grouped tracks | Guaranteed through cumulative scene description |
| The captives went to the mine | forced-march blood trail, mine traffic | Guaranteed |
| Conversion is physical and demonic | wrong wound, black-red runoff, first impaled victim | Impaled victim guaranteed |
| The thralls coordinate without speech | mine patrol behavior | Guaranteed at mine |
| The captain is controlled differently | he speaks; thralls do not; ledger orders precede his commands | Guaranteed in climax |
| The operation extends beyond Harthrun | allied command language, network notes, demon priorities | Optional continuation clue |

## Failure Handling

- A failed search roll costs time, creates exposure, or gives incomplete detail; it does not erase the mineward trail.
- If players overlook population counting, show empty occupied homes and possessions prepared for people who are absent.
- If they skip the constabulary position, the Fort Dios reply remains undiscovered; the moral question can still surface through survivors.
- If they destroy the ledger without studying it, they contain one node but lose evidence of the wider operation.

## The Minimum Forward Chain

Too few bodies → people were taken → forced movement leads to the mine.

Everything else deepens interpretation, tactics, or aftermath.
