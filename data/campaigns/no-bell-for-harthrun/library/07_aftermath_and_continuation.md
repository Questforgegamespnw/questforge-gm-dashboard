# Aftermath and Continuation

Run the aftermath in about five minutes unless the table wants a longer reckoning.

## Three Closing Questions

### Who Came Out?

Use Conversion Progress, the lower-mine choices, and the chamber outcome. Separate:

- untouched captives rescued;
- newly impaled victims saved through critical intervention;
- victims who died after removal;
- fully converted thralls;
- the subsumed captain's final state.

### What Happened to the Ledger?

- **Contained:** linked copies are isolated and the fort is warned not to read, copy, or answer them.
- **Locally Destroyed:** the chamber book is gone, but propagated links may remain.
- **Captured for Study:** valuable evidence creates continuing exposure risk.
- **Transmitted:** a report or copied order unknowingly carries the breach toward Fort Dios.

### What Does the Report Say?

- **Full truth:** Fort Dios's triage decision, false-friendly attack, conversion site, and communication threat are all recorded.
- **Protective framing:** the report acknowledges the refusal but emphasizes the information and pressures available at the time.
- **Sanitized record:** the refusal or ledger risk is omitted, protecting confidence while weakening future preparedness.

## Human Button

A survivor asks: **“We sent word. Did they ever answer?”**

Do not supply the party's response. That answer is the emotional ending.

## Continuation Hooks

Use only if desired:

- another allied ledger produces phrasing found in the chamber;
- a converted patrol is already moving through the Darksen;
- the demon escaped after transmitting a final instruction;
- the captain survives with fragments of routes, officers, or commands used by the Legion;
- Fort Dios must quarantine its own communication system while multiple fronts remain active.
