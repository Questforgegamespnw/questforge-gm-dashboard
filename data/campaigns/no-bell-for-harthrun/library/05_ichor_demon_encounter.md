# Ichor Demon Encounter

## Encounter Identity

The Ichor Demon is a battlefield commander, not merely a large damage source. It treats the chamber as an active system composed of captives, spikes, silent thralls, the subsumed captain, and the ledger network.

Use an appropriate level 8–10 fiend or demon stat block as the mechanical chassis, then add the encounter behaviors below. Exact numbers can be tuned to party size and system familiarity.

## Priorities

1. Preserve the ledger connection.
2. Keep conversion active.
3. Use the captain's familiar authority to control tempo.
4. Deploy thralls to separate rescuers from attackers.
5. Enter direct combat when command infrastructure fails.

## Battlefield Actions

- **Written Command:** A short order appears in the ledger and targets an exposed creature, thrall group, or command channel.
- **Silent Reposition:** Thralls move together to block an exit, surround a healer, or carry a captive toward a spike.
- **Spike Growth:** An empty spike erupts or extends to create hazardous terrain, pin a route, or threaten an adjacent captive.
- **Reclaim the Captain:** When his geas breaks, the demon attempts immediate impalement.
- **Blood Weapon:** In direct battle, the demon shapes or wields a brutal ichor-grown greatsword or spike blade.

## Encounter Phases

### Phase One — Command at Distance

The captain speaks; thralls act; the demon remains partially concealed. Let players recognize that the captain and ledger form a control chain.

### Phase Two — Network Disruption

When the party damages the ledger link, frees the captain, or breaks the chamber's control rhythm, the demon intervenes physically. Fire **The Demon Reclaims Him** when appropriate.

### Phase Three — Direct Contest

The demon fights to preserve a route of propagation, not necessarily to the death. If destroyed, local command collapses. If escape is more useful, it may abandon the chamber after transmitting or protecting a final command.

## Multi-Objective Victory

Damage alone should not determine the ending. Count success across:

- demon defeated or driven away;
- unprocessed captives protected;
- active conversions interrupted;
- captain recovered, released, or prevented from becoming a new thrall;
- ledger network properly contained.

Scale difficulty with numbers of thralls and the current Alert tier, not by making the demon arbitrarily tougher.
