# The Ichor Demon

*Huge fiend (demon), chaotic evil — set-piece solo boss for four 10th-level characters (approximately CR 14)*

> **Encounter identity:** Wounding the demon spills living ichor into the chamber. Those pools become routes, spawning grounds, and impalement sites. At half health, the demon recalls every pool it can still use, healing itself and resetting the battlefield. The party wins cleanly by controlling the spilled ichor—not merely by racing its hit points.

---

## At-a-Glance Run Order

1. **Initiative 20:** Choose one lair action. Do not repeat the previous round's option.
2. **Demon's turn:** Strike, command, or relocate. Set up restrained targets rather than always maximizing damage.
3. **Health threshold crossed:** Create a new ichor pool, once per turn at most.
4. **End of another creature's turn:** Spend up to 3 legendary actions.
5. **First time at 150 HP or fewer:** Recall all usable pools and begin Phase Two.
6. **Impaled creature's turn:** Apply 10 necrotic damage and advance its conversion clock.

## The Ichor Demon

**Armor Class** 18 (maintained ichor form)  
**Hit Points** 300  
**Speed** 40 ft., climb 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 22 (+6) | 14 (+2) | 22 (+6) | 14 (+2) | 18 (+4) | 20 (+5) |

**Saving Throws** Str +11, Con +11, Wis +9, Cha +10  
**Skills** Athletics +11, Perception +9  
**Damage Resistances** cold, fire, necrotic; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks  
**Damage Immunities** poison, psychic  
**Condition Immunities** charmed, frightened, poisoned, prone  
**Senses** blindsight 60 ft., tremorsense 120 ft., passive Perception 19  
**Languages** understands Abyssal and Common; communicates only through Manifest Command  
**Challenge** approximately 14 (set-piece encounter)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +5

### Traits

**Faceless Awareness.** The demon has no eyes and cannot be blinded. It perceives creatures touching the cavern floor with its tremorsense and perceives normally within its blindsight.

**Legendary Resistance (3/Day).** If the demon fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

**Magic Resistance.** The demon has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Form Held by Will.** The demon cannot be unwillingly transformed. Its liquid body can move through an opening as narrow as 1 inch wide without squeezing, but it cannot end its movement there.

**Radiant Disruption.** When the demon takes radiant damage, it cannot use Flow Through Ichor until the end of its next turn. It also cannot use Recall the Spilled Blood as a reaction to that instance of damage; resolve the recall after the next source of damage that leaves it at 150 hit points or fewer.

**Shed Ichor.** The demon creates one ichor pool in an unoccupied space within 10 feet of itself whenever its hit points first cross one of the following thresholds. It can create no more than one pool on a turn.

- **Phase One:** 270, 240, 210, 180, and 150 hit points.
- **Phase Two:** 200, 175, 150, 125, 100, 75, 50, and 25 hit points.

If several thresholds are crossed on one turn, create one pool immediately and one additional pool at the end of each of the demon's subsequent turns until all crossed thresholds have resolved or five active pools exist. A pool suppressed when Recall the Spilled Blood occurs remains on the field but is not absorbed.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** The demon makes two Ichor Greatsword attacks. It may replace one attack with Manifest Command.

**Ichor Greatsword.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +11 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. *Hit:* 17 (2d10 + 6) slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) necrotic damage. The sword pours from the demon's arm or gathers into its hand from nearby ichor; disarming it has no lasting effect.

**Manifest Command.** One creature the demon can perceive within 60 feet must succeed on a DC 18 Wisdom saving throw or immediately use its reaction to obey one of the commands below. This is a psychic effect, but it is not speech and does not require the target to understand a language.

- **Approach.** Move up to your speed toward a point the demon chooses, using the safest apparent route.
- **Drop.** Drop one held object.
- **Halt.** Your speed becomes 0 until the end of your next turn.
- **Kneel.** Fall prone.
- **Seize.** Attempt to grapple one creature within your reach.

The word is not heard. It simply becomes present inside the target's mind with the momentary certainty that it must be obeyed. A creature immune to being charmed has advantage on this save but is not automatically immune.

**Overwhelming Imperative (Recharge 5–6).** Up to three creatures the demon can perceive within 60 feet must make the saving throw against Manifest Command. The demon gives the same command to every target.

**Flow Through Ichor.** The demon collapses into one usable ichor pool it occupies and emerges from another usable pool within 90 feet. It appears in an unoccupied space adjacent to the destination pool. This movement does not provoke opportunity attacks.

### Reaction: Phase Transition

**Recall the Spilled Blood (1/Encounter).** When the demon is first reduced to 150 hit points or fewer, it recalls every active ichor pool that is not destroyed, purified, frozen, or otherwise suppressed. The demon regains 15 hit points for each pool absorbed, to a maximum of 75 hit points, and each absorbed pool disappears.

Every creature standing in a recalled pool must succeed on a DC 18 Strength saving throw or be pulled up to 15 feet toward the demon and knocked prone. On a failure by 5 or more, it is also grappled by the demon's ichor (escape DC 18) until the end of the demon's next turn.

Recall does not absorb a pool supporting an impalement spike. A suppressed pool contributes no healing and remains where it is. Once Recall resolves, Phase Two begins and the Phase Two shedding thresholds become active.

> **Read aloud — Recall:** Every pool in the cavern draws tight around its edges. Then the spilled ichor moves—streaming uphill, crawling across walls, and dragging itself through cracks in branching black-red veins. The demon opens its arms as the blood strikes it from every direction, rebuilding the shape it has chosen. A single command becomes manifest inside your thoughts: **RETURN.**

### Legendary Actions

The demon can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The demon regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

**Flowing Step (1 Action).** The demon moves up to half its speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

**Ichor Greatsword (1 Action).** The demon makes one Ichor Greatsword attack.

**Manifest Command (1 Action).** The demon uses Manifest Command.

**Rise, Lesser Form (2 Actions).** One usable ichor pool produces an ichor figure in an unoccupied space within it. No more than four ichor figures may exist at once.

**Sudden Impalement (2 Actions).** The demon uses Impaling Growth, originating from one usable ichor pool.

**Flow Through Ichor (2 Actions).** The demon uses Flow Through Ichor.

## Lair Actions: The Expanded Cavern

On initiative count 20 (losing initiative ties), the demon takes one lair action. It cannot choose the same option two rounds in a row.

**Impaling Growth.** A spike erupts from a usable ichor pool. One creature within 5 feet of that pool must make a DC 18 Dexterity saving throw, with disadvantage if it is grappled or restrained. On a failure, it takes 18 (4d8) piercing damage plus 9 (2d8) necrotic damage and becomes impaled. On a success, it takes half as much damage and is not impaled.

**Lesser Forms Rise.** One usable pool produces one ichor figure, subject to the four-figure maximum.

**The Ichor Answers.** Each usable pool spreads 5 feet, to a maximum radius of 10 feet. A creature other than an ichor creature caught in the expansion must succeed on a DC 18 Strength saving throw or its speed becomes 0 until initiative count 20 of the next round.

**Imperative Made Flesh.** The demon uses Manifest Command against each creature standing in or adjacent to one pool. A creature that succeeds is immune to this lair action until initiative count 20 of the next round.

**Drink the Fallen.** The demon consumes one usable pool within 90 feet and regains 12 hit points. The pool disappears. A pool supporting an impalement spike cannot be consumed.

## Ichor Pools

An ichor pool is a 10-foot-radius area of living black-red fluid unless it has spread. It is difficult terrain for creatures other than the demon and its ichor figures. The demon can perceive through every usable pool as though occupying its space.

**Pool Object Statistics:** AC 12; 20 hit points; immunity to necrotic, poison, and psychic damage. A pool automatically fails Strength and Dexterity saving throws.

The party can neutralize pools in several ways:

- **Destroy:** Reduce the pool to 0 hit points. Weapon attacks must be plausibly capable of dispersing or removing liquid; the GM may rule that a narrow piercing weapon cannot damage it.
- **Purify:** Holy water destroys a pool it directly strikes. A spell or feature that consecrates, purifies, or removes supernatural corruption destroys a pool if the GM judges it sufficiently potent.
- **Radiant:** A pool that takes radiant damage becomes unusable until initiative count 20 of the next round. If it takes 15 or more radiant damage from one effect, it is destroyed.
- **Freeze:** A pool that takes 10 or more cold damage from one effect freezes and becomes unusable until initiative count 20 of the next round. A frozen pool is solid ground and can be destroyed normally.
- **Thunder:** A pool that takes 15 or more thunder damage from one effect cannot spawn a figure or form a spike until initiative count 20 of the next round, though the demon may still travel through it.

An **unusable** pool cannot spawn an ichor figure, originate a spike, serve as a destination for Flow Through Ichor, be consumed by Drink the Fallen, or contribute healing to Recall the Spilled Blood.

## Ichor Figure

*Medium fiend, unaligned*

**Armor Class** 13  
**Hit Points** 18 (4d8)  
**Speed** 30 ft., climb 20 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 16 (+3) | 12 (+1) | 10 (+0) | 3 (−4) | 10 (+0) | 3 (−4) |

**Damage Resistances** bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks  
**Damage Immunities** poison, psychic  
**Condition Immunities** charmed, frightened, poisoned, prone  
**Senses** blindsight 30 ft., passive Perception 10  
**Languages** —

**Pool-Born.** The figure ignores difficult terrain created by ichor pools. If it ends its turn more than 60 feet from an active pool, it loses cohesion and dies.

**Clinging Strike.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (1d10 + 3) bludgeoning damage, and the target is grappled (escape DC 15). Until the grapple ends, the figure cannot use Clinging Strike against another target.

**Smother (Requires a Grappled Target).** The grappled creature must succeed on a DC 15 Strength or Dexterity saving throw (its choice) or become restrained until the grapple ends. A creature restrained this way may repeat the save at the end of each of its turns.

**Collapse.** When the figure dies, its space becomes sticky difficult terrain until initiative count 20 of the next round. It does not create a new ichor pool.

> **Run the adds as hands, not soldiers.** They grapple, drag, and restrain targets for Impaling Growth. They should rarely spend actions attacking a creature they cannot help control.

## Impalement and Conversion

An impaled creature is restrained, has speed 0, and cannot be moved away from the spike. At the start of each of its turns, it takes 10 (3d6) necrotic damage and advances one step on the conversion clock.

### Freeing an Impaled Creature

A creature within reach may use its action to attempt one of the following:

- **Pull Free:** DC 18 Strength (Athletics). On a success, the victim is freed and takes 7 (2d6) slashing damage.
- **Careful Extraction:** DC 18 Wisdom (Medicine). On a success, the victim is freed without additional damage.
- **Destroy or Purify the Pool:** The spike collapses and releases the victim without additional damage.
- **Magical Rescue:** Teleportation, forced movement, or comparable magic works only if it can carry the victim and the impaling spike together or first severs the spike. The GM should reward a clearly applicable spell rather than using this clause to invalidate it.

Once freed, the creature's conversion progress resets after it receives magical healing or a creature succeeds on a DC 15 Wisdom (Medicine) check to purge and bind the wound. Lesser restoration or comparable magic automatically purges it.

### Six-Round Conversion Clock

| Step | Effect |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Black-red veins spread around the wound. No additional mechanical effect. |
| 2 | The victim feels distant commands forming at the edge of thought. |
| 3 | The victim has disadvantage on saves against Manifest Command. |
| 4 | The victim cannot take reactions and hears the demon's commands continuously. |
| 5 | At the start of its turn, the victim must succeed on a DC 18 Wisdom save or lose either its action or movement that turn (victim's choice). |
| 6 | At the end of the victim's turn, conversion completes. The victim becomes a thrall under the demon's control until the ichor is removed by greater restoration, heal, or similarly powerful magic. |

Conversion is deliberately slow. It should occur only if the party leaves someone impaled for approximately six rounds; its primary purpose is rescue pressure and visible horror, not sudden character loss.

## Reveal and Combat Behavior

The demon is not visible when the party enters the chamber. It remains concealed as dispersed ichor within fissures, runoff, and shadowed stone until it chooses to strike. Psychic commands may manifest before its body does.

> **Read aloud — Manifestation:** The darkness behind the captain moves without advancing. Black-red liquid draws itself out of cracks in the stone, rising in sheets that refuse to fall. It gathers into a towering human shape—solid only because something inside it insists that it remain so. Its surface ripples and reforms around a face that does not exist. Two shallow indentations suggest eyes. A deeper hollow suggests a mouth. None of them open. The ichor along one arm lengthens, hardens, and remembers the shape of a greatsword.

### Tactical Priorities

- **Opening:** Manifest with a strike or command; establish that the captain was never the true threat.
- **Control:** Use commands and figures to place targets beside pools.
- **Punish:** Impale restrained or isolated targets, leaving the party a full rescue window.
- **Mobility:** Relocate when surrounded, but do not nullify every melee commitment.
- **Recall:** At half health, sacrifice the established network for recovery and a clean Phase Two reset.
- **Phase Two:** Shed pools more rapidly, force the party to apply the counterplay they learned, and become less conservative with legendary attacks.

The demon does not taunt, bargain, roar, or speak aloud. Its only “voice” is psychic certainty made manifest as command.

## Tuning Levers

Use the printed block first. Adjust only if the party's composition or resources clearly demand it.

| Desired pressure | Adjustment |
|---|---|
| Easier / depleted party | DC 17; maximum 3 figures; recall heals 10 HP per usable pool; remove Magic Resistance. |
| Baseline | DC 18; maximum 4 figures; recall heals 15 HP per usable pool (maximum 75). |
| Strong / optimized party | 330 starting HP; maximum 5 figures; demon gains 4 legendary actions during Phase Two. |
| Faster session | 260 starting HP; use only four Phase One pool thresholds; maximum recall 60 HP. |

### GM Fairness Rules

- Telegraph which pool is about to form a spike whenever practical.
- Never complete conversion early because of a failed save or critical hit.
- Do not repeatedly impale the same newly freed character unless the battlefield naturally leaves no better target.
- Let creative radiant, cold, purification, and thunder effects interact with pools even when a spell was not written for this exact purpose.
- Remember that consuming or recalling pools costs the demon its routes, adds, and impalement sites. Healing is a trade, not free recovery.
