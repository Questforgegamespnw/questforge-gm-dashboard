# The Gilded Harvest — Inventory Audit

## Current Adventure Spine

### Core Premise

A tired adventuring party returns from a weeklong drake hunt and is assigned what should be an easy festival security contract. The Gilded Harvest is a major agritourism event where rival farming families compete for economically vital prize money. One family has dominated the grand crop competition for five years by exploiting a fae loophole: their crops are not magically altered, but their field itself is partially Feywild.

The elegant wife of the winning farmer is actually a fae queen/matron using the festival as a mass micro-bargain engine through bespoke candles. Her consort in the Feywild believes she has “gone native” and is testing her control by stealing the prize crops early, hoping to prove she should be deposed.
