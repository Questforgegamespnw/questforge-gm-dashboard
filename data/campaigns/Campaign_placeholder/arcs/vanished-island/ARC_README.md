# Arc Template

Rename `_ARC_SLUG_` to the new arc folder name, such as `ashen-root`.


Core rules:

- Keep always-on campaign systems in the campaign's own `global/` folder when one exists.
- Keep content reachable during this arc inside this arc folder.
- Do not import campaign-specific content from another campaign.
- Keep public collection names generic: `actors`, `locations`, `factions`, `threads`, `trackers`, `scenes`, `fireableMoments`, `tables`, `references`, and `ambientCast`.

This becomes the human-readable source of truth for everything currently remembered.

The strongest re-entry is not merely “merfolk are attacking.”

It is:

The party breaks through the jungle expecting that only minutes have passed. The sailors aboard the ship look exhausted, wounded, and terrified—as though they have been fighting for hours.

That immediately demonstrates the time distortion through consequences rather than exposition, makes the old session easy to remember, and gives the campaign a sharp opening crisis.
